## Understanding Beast of Reincarnation Steam Deck Performance

The Steam Deck is an incredible device for playing Beast of Reincarnation, but it requires careful tuning to avoid performance drops during boss fights. While the game is not officially verified, community testing has shown that a locked 40 FPS is entirely achievable with the right configuration. The goal is to balance visual fidelity with stable performance, because the game's fast-paced combat demands consistent frame times.

The primary bottleneck on the Steam Deck is the GPU during heavy particle effects, particularly during the Beast's **Rebirth Cycle** phase. Adjusting in-game settings alone is not enough; you must also leverage the Deck's built-in performance overlay and TDP controls. The following table outlines the recommended starting point for a smooth experience.

| Setting | Recommended Value | Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frame Rate Limit** | 40 FPS | Eliminates frame pacing issues common at 60 FPS |
| **Refresh Rate** | 40 Hz | Matches the frame cap for smooth frame delivery |
| **TDP Limit** | 9 Watts | Reduces fan noise and extends battery life without performance loss |
| **GPU Clock Speed** | 1200 MHz | Provides a stable core clock to prevent dynamic dips |
| **Scaling Filter** | FSR | Sharpens the image when running at lower internal resolutions |

In-game, you should set the **Shadow Quality** to Low and **Anti-Aliasing** to Medium. These two settings have the biggest impact on GPU headroom during combat. Additionally, ensure you are running the game from the internal SSD, as running from a microSD card can introduce stuttering during asset streaming when transitioning between areas. Mastering these technical fundamentals allows you to focus on high-level combat strategies, much like those discussed in our [Beast of Reincarnation PC controls optimization guide](/en/platforms/beast-of-reincarnation-pc-troubleshooting/).

### Optimizing Battery Life for Long Sessions

A single charge can last for hours if you are willing to make small compromises. The goal is to reduce the power draw of the APU without sacrificing the responsiveness needed for parrying. The **TDP Limit** is your most powerful tool here.

- **30 FPS Exploration**: Set a per-game profile to lock at 30 FPS for story segments.
- **40 FPS Combat**: Quickly switch to a 40 FPS profile when entering a boss arena.
- **Half Rate Shading**: Enable this in the Quick Access Menu for a 5-8% performance uplift in dense forests.

## Mastering Beast of Reincarnation PS5 Pro Guide

The PS5 Pro offers a transformative experience for Beast of Reincarnation, leveraging the extra GPU power to deliver a rock-solid frame rate. The standard PS5 already runs the game well, but the Pro allows for a true 4K experience with enhanced ray-traced reflections in the cursed water sections. This is the definitive console version, and using a **Beast of Reincarnation PS5 Pro guide** will ensure you are extracting every ounce of visual fidelity.

While the PS5 Pro brute-forces performance, the game still requires you to manually select your preferred mode. The game defaults to a **Quality Mode** that targets 30 FPS with full ray tracing. However, for a game that requires split-second reactions, a **Performance Mode** is highly recommended. The table below breaks down the two primary modes.

| Feature | Quality Mode | Performance Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target Resolution** | 4K (3840x2160) | 1440p upscaled to 4K |
| **Target Frame Rate** | 30 FPS | 60 FPS |
| **Ray Tracing** | Full (Reflections + Shadows) | Limited (Reflections only) |
| **Visual Trade-off** | Maximum fidelity | Minor reduction in shadow quality |
| **Smoothness** | Consistent 30 FPS with occasional dips | Locked 60 FPS |

The **PS5 frame rate** is incredibly stable in Performance Mode, rarely deviating from the 60 FPS target even during the most chaotic multi-enemy encounters. You can further reduce input lag by ensuring your TV is set to **Game Mode**. This disables post-processing effects that can add 50-100ms of delay, which is the difference between a successful dodge and taking a direct hit.

### Controller Customization and DualSense Features

The DualSense controller’s haptic feedback and adaptive triggers are fully utilized in Beast of Reincarnation. You can feel the tension of your bowstring through the triggers, and distinct haptic pulses indicate low health or a fully charged spirit gauge. However, competitive players often disable the adaptive triggers for a faster trigger response.

To customize this, navigate to the Accessories menu in your PS5 settings and create a custom profile. Turn the **Trigger Effect Intensity** to Off for L2 and R2. This ensures that your attack commands are registered instantly without the physical resistance of the adaptive trigger mechanism. The game also supports full **controller support PC** features when using a DualSense on a computer, but you will need a wired connection to access all of the advanced haptics.

## Configuring Beast of Reincarnation Controller Support PC

Playing with a controller on PC is the preferred method for many players, as the game was designed with a gamepad in mind. Native **Beast of Reincarnation controller support PC** is robust, but it is not entirely plug-and-play if you want the best experience. The game supports both Xbox and PlayStation controllers, but they behave very differently out of the box.

Xbox controllers work immediately, recognized by Windows and Steam Input without any extra configuration. PlayStation controllers, however, present a choice: you can either let Steam Input handle the translation to XInput or use the game's native support for the PlayStation touchpad and gyro. Disabling Steam Input for the game in its properties menu is the only way to get native PlayStation prompts and gyro functionality. The following table clarifies the input methods.

| Controller Type | Steam Input Status | In-Game Prompts | Gyro Support |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Xbox Series X/S | Enabled (Default) | Xbox Buttons | No (unless configured manually) |
| DualSense | Disabled (Forced Off) | PlayStation Buttons | Yes (Native) |
| DualShock 4 | Disabled (Forced Off) | PlayStation Buttons | Yes (Native) |
| Switch Pro | Enabled (Default) | Generic Buttons | Yes (via Steam Input) |

To get your PlayStation controller working natively, right-click the game in your Steam library, select `Properties` > `Controller`, and change `Override for Beast of Reincarnation` to `Disable Steam Input`. After this, launch the game, and you will see the correct button icons. The gyro function can then be activated in the game's own settings, allowing precise aiming without stick aiming.

### Creating Custom Action Layers

The default control scheme is functional but inefficient for advanced techniques like animation canceling. By creating a custom action layer, you can move critical actions to more accessible buttons. This is done directly in the Steam controller configurator.

A popular configuration among players is to map the **Dash** action to the left paddle and the **Interact** action to the right paddle. This allows you to keep both thumbs on the analog sticks at all times, essential for maintaining camera control during intense combat. This is the first step professional players take when optimizing their setup for a flawless run.

## Beast of Reincarnation Keyboard Mouse Guide for Precision

While controllers offer comfort, a **Beast of Reincarnation keyboard mouse guide** reveals the undeniable truth: a mouse provides superior camera control and aiming precision. The game’s default keybindings are adequate, but they suffer from a critical flaw—they force your left hand into uncomfortable positions. Remapping the core actions is mandatory for long play sessions.

The single most important rebind is moving the **Dash** action off of the Shift key. Holding Shift while trying to press other keys causes fatigue very quickly. Instead, map Dash to a mouse side button or to the Spacebar, and move Jump to the other. The table below provides a recommended setup that reduces hand strain and increases actions per minute.

| Function | Default Key | Recommended Key | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dash** | Left Shift | Mouse Button 4 | Frees up left hand for movement |
| **Jump** | Space | Space | Keep as default |
| **Light Attack** | Left Click | Left Click | Keep as default |
| **Heavy Attack** | Right Click | Right Click | Keep as default |
| **Lock-On** | Middle Mouse | Mouse Button 5 | Easier to toggle without disrupting aim |
| **Use Item** | R | Q | Quick access for healing |

You must also adjust the in-game mouse smoothing slider. Set it to 0 to ensure raw input, eliminating any acceleration that interferes with muscle memory. This is a standard practice for any pro-level player.

### Advanced KBM Settings for Competitive Play

Beyond simple remapping, the key to high-level play is mastering the **Lock-On** system with a mouse. You should configure a DPI switch on your mouse to drop sensitivity instantly when locking onto a boss. This allows for rapid wide sweeps for exploration and pixel-precise tracking during combat.

Furthermore, you can set macros for specific ability combinations, but be warned that the game's anti-cheat may flag complex sequences. A safer alternative is to use the in-game keybind settings to assign secondary keys for your most-used spirit abilities. Placing these on the number keys or a gaming mouse’s 12-button grid ensures you never have to stop moving to cast a spell.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I activate controller support for Beast of Reincarnation on PC?**

You can use any modern gamepad, but for native PlayStation prompts and gyro aim, you must disable Steam Input in the game's properties menu. Xbox controllers work instantly with the default Steam Input configuration and provide the most hassle-free experience.

**What is the best PS5 frame rate mode for Beast of Reincarnation?**

The Performance Mode is recommended for a locked 60 FPS experience, which is crucial for timing parries against the Beast. Quality Mode offers higher resolution and ray tracing, but the 30 FPS target is a noticeable disadvantage in combat.

**Can the Steam Deck run Beast of Reincarnation at 60 FPS?**

Stable 60 FPS is difficult to achieve on the Steam Deck without making the game look significantly worse. A locked 40 FPS provides the best balance of visual clarity, input response, and battery life for handheld play.

**Why do my keyboard controls feel sluggish in Beast of Reincarnation?**

The default mouse smoothing settings introduce input delay. Navigate to the game's control settings and set the **Mouse Smoothing** slider to 0 to enable raw mouse input, which will provide a 1:1 translation of your physical movements.

**Does the PS5 Pro version of Beast of Reincarnation have ray tracing?**

Yes, the PS5 Pro version features enhanced ray-traced reflections in water-heavy areas, alongside a more stable frame rate. This is the definitive console experience for players prioritizing visual fidelity.
