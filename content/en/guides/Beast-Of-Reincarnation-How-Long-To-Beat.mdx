| Stat | Priority | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Stamina Regeneration** | High | Allows for more dodges and charged attacks during critical windows. |

Instead, focus on maximizing your damage output to end fights quickly, which is the most effective form of damage mitigation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does it take to beat Beast of Reincarnation 100%?**

A 100% completion run, including all endings and achievements, takes between 80 and 100 hours for most players. This estimate includes the time required to farm for rare beast forms like the **Celestial Dragon** and to complete the game on the hardest difficulty tier.

**Is Beast of Reincarnation harder than other games in the genre?**

The game is considered moderately difficult, with a steep initial learning curve that levels out as you master the rebirth mechanics. The difficulty spikes are significant, but the power growth from reincarnation makes subsequent cycles much smoother.

**Can I miss any endings in a single playthrough?**

Yes, you cannot see all endings in a single playthrough because the **True Awakening** ending requires starting a new cycle with specific conditions met. You must plan your Beast of Reincarnation all endings guide routing carefully to minimize the number of full loops needed.

**What is the fastest possible completion time for the main story?**

A skilled player on a New Game+ cycle can complete the main story in approximately 8 hours by skipping all optional content and using optimized boss-killing strategies. For a first-time player, expecting a sub-20 hour clear is unrealistic.

**Does the game have missable content?**

Yes, several **Memory Shards** and beast forms are permanently missable if you progress past certain story points without fulfilling specific conditions. This is why a completionist run requires careful planning and multiple playthroughs to experience everything the game has to offer.
