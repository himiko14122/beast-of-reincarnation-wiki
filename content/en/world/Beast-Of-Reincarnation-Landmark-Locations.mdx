The **Miasma Tide** is a dynamic weather event specific to the Far East, which drastically reduces visibility and spawns elite **Remnant Samurai**—enemies that are near-impossible to kill with a level 1 Emma. According to community data, waiting out the tide inside the Silo is the only safe method for a fresh player, while speedrunners have been reported to use the **Faded Torii Gate** at the northernmost cliff to despawn aggressive mobs instantly.

### Mastering the Dual-Mobility System for Exploration

A common mistake new players make is treating Koo as a purely combat-focused companion. His **Spectral Path** ability, unlocked after cleansing the **Shrine of the First Sin**, creates temporary platforms over blighted chasms, which are the key to bypassing large sections of the Western Citadels. For example, reaching the **Sky-Piercing Needle**, a colossal weapon embedded in the center of the map, is only possible by combining Emma's chain-dash with a perfectly timed Spectral Path to bridge a gap that the chain cannot cross on its own.

- **Chain-Dash into Spectral Path**: Use Emma’s dash to gain forward momentum, then switch to Koo mid-air to cast a bridge, extending your reach.
- **Spectral Path Climbing**: In the Northern Barrens, you can cast a Spectral Path at a 45-degree angle against ice walls to create a ramp, bypassing blocked caverns.
- **Ruined Shrines**: Interacting with these with Koo reveals hidden **Memory Echoes**, which are not just lore but also mark unmarked treasure caches on your map.
