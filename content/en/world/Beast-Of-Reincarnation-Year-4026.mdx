The **Toxic Swamps** are the most dangerous but also the most rewarding. Here, you will find the rare **Bile-Covered Ore** nodes needed for high-level weapon crafting. Navigating the swamps requires the **Swamp Walker's Charm**, a key item dropped by a mini-boss in the **Sunken Pagoda**.
