Castella, the Windrunner of DragonSword: Awakening, is a ranged magic-user whose combat revolves around the Airborne Status Ailment and the Gale element. She is the third pillar of the game's most powerful Stun Lock composition alongside Lute and Dana, and her ability to launch enemies into the air creates unique combo opportunities that no other hero can replicate. Castella's playstyle is distinct from melee heroes like Lute and Johnny — she attacks from mid-range, excels at hitting multiple enemies simultaneously, and her Airborne ailment creates an extended vulnerability window that makes tag combo execution more forgiving.

This guide covers both primary Castella build archetypes: the Airborne Control build optimized for Stun Lock compositions, and the Gale DPS build that maximizes her raw damage output. We will break down her skill tree, equipment options, Familiar pairings, and tag combo rotations in detail.

## Castella's Core Mechanics: Airborne and Gale Synergy

Castella's primary Status Ailment is Airborne, which launches enemies into the air and renders them unable to attack or move for the duration. Airborne is unique among the five Status Ailments because it physically displaces the enemy, creating a visual and mechanical window that is longer and more distinct than other crowd-control effects. When an enemy is Airborne, they float helplessly for approximately 2 seconds at base duration, during which any attack against them gains a damage bonus.

The Gale element is Castella's secondary mechanic. Many of her skills deal Gale-type damage, which has a natural chance to trigger a "Gale Gust" effect — a small knockback that interrupts enemy animations. While Gale Gust is not the same as the Airborne Status Ailment, it provides incidental crowd control that makes Castella's gameplay feel fluid and controlling even before she intentionally triggers Airborne.

Castella's basic attack string consists of four ranged Gale bolts. Without build investment, Airborne is triggered on the fourth hit. Like Lute's Knockdown, the goal of any Castella build is to reduce this threshold and maximize the value of each Airborne trigger.

### Airborne and Tag Combo Interaction

Airborne is the most valuable Status Ailment for tag combo execution because the extended vulnerability window gives you more time to execute Switching Signals. When Castella applies Airborne and her Signal Skill fires, the Switching Signal window is approximately 30% longer than the window created by Knockdown or Freeze. This means tagging from Castella to another hero is more forgiving, reducing the chance of dropping a combo chain.

Additionally, enemies that are Airborne take 15% more damage from all sources, a passive bonus that stacks with Signal Skill damage multipliers. This makes Castella an excellent setup hero — she launches the enemy, and the next hero in the tag chain deals amplified damage.

## Airborne Control Build: Stun Lock Third Pillar

The Airborne Control build is Castella's most popular and effective archetype. It optimizes Airborne duration and application speed to make Castella the third pillar of the Dana + Lute + Castella Stun Lock composition. In this role, Castella is the hero who extends the crowd-control chain between Dana's Freeze and Lute's Knockdown, ensuring enemies never get a chance to recover.

### Skill Tree Path for Airborne Control Castella

Invest skill points in the following priority order:

1. **Gale Force (5 ranks)** — Reduces the number of hits required to trigger Airborne from 4 to 2. This is the most impactful node for Castella because it more than doubles her Airborne application speed. At max rank, Castella can trigger Airborne with just two basic attacks, making her the fastest ailment applicator in the game.
2. **Airborne Duration Extension (5 ranks)** — Extends the base duration of Airborne by 0.4 seconds per rank. At max rank, Airborne lasts an additional 2 seconds, for a total base duration of approximately 4 seconds. Combined with equipment bonuses, this makes Airborne the longest crowd-control ailment in the game.
3. **Ailment Overlap: Airborne Extension (3 ranks)** — When Airborne is applied while another Status Ailment is active, Airborne duration is extended by an additional 20%. In a Stun Lock team, Castella always tags in after Lute's Knockdown or Dana's Freeze, so this bonus is consistently active.
4. **Switching Signal Window Extension (3 ranks)** — Extends the tag window after Castella's Signal Skill by 0.3 seconds per rank. Because Castella's Airborne already provides a generous window, this investment makes tag combos nearly impossible to drop.
5. **Gale Gust Enhancement (2 ranks)** — Increases the knockback distance of Gale Gust, providing additional crowd control between intentional Airborne triggers. This is a quality-of-life node that makes Castella's basic gameplay feel smoother.

Total investment is 18 skill points for the core path. The remaining points can go into minor survivability nodes or the Gale DPS branch if you want a hybrid build.

### Equipment for Airborne Control Castella

Weapon selection for Airborne Control Castella centers on the Zephyr Wand line, which provides passive bonuses to Gale element attacks and Airborne application speed. The Zephyr Wand of the Storm, obtained from the Skypeak Ruins dungeon, is the best-in-slot weapon because it adds a 12% chance to apply Airborne on any hit, similar to the Flamberge's bonus for Lute's Knockdown.

Armor should prioritize ailment duration and ability cooldown reduction. The Windweaver's Mantle reduces the cooldown of Castella's "Tempest Spiral" skill by 15%, allowing more frequent use of her area-of-effect Gale attack. The Entropy Gauntlets, which increase all ailment durations by 10%, are a universal choice for any Stun Lock build. The accessory slot should hold the Chainbreaker Medallion for the same reason it benefits Lute — it reduces the Airborne hit threshold when another Status Ailment is active, making Castella even faster in team compositions.

### Familiar Pairing for Airborne Control Castella

The Wind Sprite Familiar is the optimal pairing for Airborne Control Castella. Its passive ability, "Zephyr's Blessing," extends the duration of Airborne by 10% whenever Castella triggers her Signal Skill. Since Castella triggers Signal Skills frequently with the Airborne Control build, this bonus has near-permanent uptime. The Wind Sprite can be found in the high-altitude regions of the Continent of Orbis and is a free Familiar available through standard exploration.

An alternative Familiar is the Storm Hawk, which provides a small boost to ailment application speed. This is useful early in the game when you have not yet maxed the Gale Force skill tree node and need help reaching the two-hit Airborne threshold.

### Airborne Control Castella Tag Combo Rotation

In the Dana + Lute + Castella Stun Lock composition, Castella's role follows a specific pattern:

1. Dana applies Freeze → tag to Lute.
2. Lute applies Knockdown (overlapping Freeze) → tag to Castella.
3. Castella applies Airborne (overlapping Knockdown) → Signal Skill "Tempest Spiral" fires.
4. Tag to Dana → Dana re-applies Freeze (overlapping Airborne).
5. Cycle repeats.

The critical timing point for Castella is step 3: she must apply Airborne before Lute's Knockdown expires. With full investment in Airborne duration and application speed, this window is comfortable — Castella needs only two hits to trigger Airborne, and the overlapping ailment bonus ensures extended duration. ## Gale DPS Build: Raw Damage Alternative

The Gale DPS build sacrifices crowd-control duration for raw damage output. This build maximizes the damage of Castella's skills rather than extending Airborne, making her a contributing DPS hero in compositions where Stun Lock is not the priority.

### Skill Tree Path for Gale DPS Castella

1. **Tempest Spiral Damage Multiplier (5 ranks)** — Increases the damage of Castella's Signal Skill "Tempest Spiral" by 8% per rank. At max rank, Tempest Spiral deals 40% more damage.
2. **Gale Force (3 ranks)** — Reduces Airborne threshold to 3 hits. Even in a DPS build, faster Airborne triggers mean more Signal Skills, so this node remains valuable.
3. **Critical Strike on Gale Skills (3 ranks)** — Adds 10% critical hit chance per rank to all Gale-based skills.
4. **Tempest Spiral Area Expansion (3 ranks)** — Increases the area of effect of Tempest Spiral by 15% per rank, hitting more enemies in group encounters.
5. **Airborne Damage Bonus Enhancement (2 ranks)** — Increases the damage bonus that enemies take while Airborne from 15% to 22%. This amplifies damage for the entire team, not just Castella.

The Gale DPS build is recommended for content where enemies die quickly and crowd control is unnecessary. In standard dungeon runs, a DPS Castella can clear waves of normal enemies rapidly with her area-of-effect Gale attacks.

## Castella's Positioning and Range Management

Unlike melee heroes, Castella operates at mid-range. Her basic attacks have approximately 50% more range than Lute's melee attacks, which means she can hit enemies without being in their attack range. However, her skills require her to be closer than her maximum basic attack range, creating a positioning tension that players must manage.

The optimal Castella positioning is just outside the enemy's melee range but within skill casting range. This allows her to apply Airborne with basic attacks from safety, then step forward to cast "Tempest Spiral" for maximum damage, then step back to safety while the tag rotation continues. This dance of positioning is what separates good Castella players from great ones. For more on combat positioning, see our [combat system guide](/en/guides/combat-system-guide/).

## Castella vs Aria: Ranged Hero Comparison

Players often ask whether Castella or Aria is the better ranged hero. The answer depends on the team composition. Castella provides Airborne crowd control, which is unique to her. Aria provides Shock crowd control and higher single-target DPS. In a Stun Lock team, Castella is superior because Airborne creates longer vulnerability windows than Shock. In a pure DPS team, Aria is superior because her damage multipliers are higher. The two heroes are not interchangeable — they serve fundamentally different roles despite both being ranged.

## FAQ

### Is Castella necessary for Stun Lock compositions?

Castella is not strictly necessary, but she is the strongest third hero for the Dana + Lute + Castella Stun Lock trio. Without Castella, you can substitute Aria (Shock) or Johnny (Shock), but the crowd-control chain is shorter because Shock has a shorter base duration than Airborne. If you do not have Castella unlocked yet, Aria is a viable substitute until you complete Castella's Hero Request questline. For more on Aria, see our [Aria hero guide](/en/heroes/aria-hero-guide/).

### How does Airborne interact with other Status Ailments?

Airborne stacks multiplicatively with other crowd-control ailments when overlapping. When an enemy is both Frozen and Airborne, the total disabled duration equals Freeze duration plus Airborne duration, because the two ailments compound rather than override each other. This is why the triple-stun composition is so powerful — each ailment adds its full duration to the total crowd-control window.

### Can Castella's Gale DPS build work in Very Hard dungeons?

The Gale DPS build can work in Very Hard dungeons, but it is less effective than the Airborne Control build because Very Hard bosses deal damage that makes crowd control more valuable than raw damage output. In Very Hard content, Castella's primary value is her Airborne duration, not her damage. If you want to run a DPS Castella in Very Hard, you need a different team composition that provides crowd control through other heroes.

### What is the best weapon for Castella?

The Zephyr Wand of the Storm is the best-in-slot weapon for Airborne Control Castella because it adds a 12% chance to apply Airborne on any hit, dramatically increasing application speed. For Gale DPS Castella, the Tempest Caller staff provides higher raw damage multipliers on Gale skills. Both weapons are obtained from different dungeons — the Zephyr Wand from Skypeak Ruins and the Tempest Caller from the Crimson Hollow.

### How do I unlock Castella through the Hero Request system?

Castella is unlocked through the "Winds of Change" Hero Request questline, which becomes available after completing the main story chapter in the Skypeak region. The quest involves defeating a Gale-element boss, collecting specific crafting materials, and completing a dialogue sequence. Once the quest is finished, Castella joins your roster permanently.
