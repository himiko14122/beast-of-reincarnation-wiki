Roxy, the dual-blade assassin of DragonSword: Awakening, is the game's premier Bleed specialist and one of the most satisfying heroes to play when built correctly. Her combat revolves around the Bleed Status Ailment — the only ailment that deals sustained damage over time rather than crowd control. This makes Roxy fundamentally different from heroes like Lute, Dana, and Castella, who use their ailments to disable enemies. Roxy uses Bleed to kill enemies, slowly and relentlessly, while her tag-team partners handle the crowd-control duties.

This guide covers both of Roxy's viable build archetypes: the Bleed DPS Burst build, which maximizes the damage of her Signal Skill by rapidly stacking Bleed, and the DOT Pressure build, which extends Bleed duration and stacks to create an attrition-based playstyle. ## Roxy's Core Mechanics: Bleed and the Dual-Blade System

Roxy's primary Status Ailment is Bleed, which deals a fixed amount of damage per second for its duration. Unlike other Status Ailments that disable enemies, Bleed is purely offensive — it does not interrupt attacks, prevent movement, or disable skills. What makes Bleed powerful is its stackability. Multiple instances of Bleed can be active on the same enemy simultaneously, and each stack deals its own damage per second independently. A Roxy with eight stacks of Bleed on a single enemy is dealing eight times the base Bleed damage per second, which is an enormous amount of sustained damage.

Roxy's dual-blade system means she attacks twice with each basic attack input — once with each blade. Each hit has an independent chance to contribute to the Bleed threshold. Without build investment, Bleed is triggered after six individual hits (three basic attack inputs × two blades each). This is slower than Lute's base five-hit threshold, which reflects the fact that Roxy's dual-blade attacks deal less damage per hit but more hits per second.

Another key mechanic is Roxy's "Crimson Spiral" Signal Skill. When Bleed is triggered, Crimson Spiral fires as a spinning dual-blade attack that deals multiple hits in rapid succession. Each hit of Crimson Spiral can apply an additional Bleed stack if the "Hemorrhage" skill tree node is invested in. This means a single Crimson Spiral can potentially add three to five additional Bleed stacks, dramatically accelerating the damage ramp.

### Bleed Damage Per Stack

The base damage of a single Bleed stack depends on Roxy's attack power. At typical mid-game values, a single Bleed stack deals approximately 2% of Roxy's attack power per second for 6 seconds. This means a single stack deals approximately 12% of her attack power in total damage over its full duration. With eight stacks, the total Bleed damage equals approximately 96% of her attack power over 6 seconds — on top of the damage from her regular attacks and Signal Skills.

## Bleed DPS Burst Build: Maximum Stack Velocity

The Bleed DPS Burst build is Roxy's most popular archetype. It maximizes the speed at which she applies Bleed stacks and the damage of her Crimson Spiral Signal Skill. The goal is to reach maximum Bleed stacks as quickly as possible and then maintain those stacks through the entire encounter, dealing sustained burst damage that rivals or exceeds Lute's Knockdown-based DPS.

### Skill Tree Path for Bleed DPS Burst Roxy

Invest skill points in the following priority order:

1. **Hemorrhage (5 ranks)** — Each hit of the Crimson Spiral Signal Skill has a 15% chance per rank to apply an additional Bleed stack. At max rank, each hit of Crimson Spiral has a 75% chance to apply an extra Bleed stack. Since Crimson Spiral hits approximately 6-8 times, this means 4-6 additional Bleed stacks per Signal Skill trigger, dramatically accelerating stack accumulation.

2. **Rapid Incision (5 ranks)** — Reduces the number of hits required to trigger Bleed from 6 to 3. This is Roxy's equivalent of Lute's Staggering Blow — the single most impactful node for her DPS because it allows her to trigger Crimson Spiral more frequently.

3. **Crimson Spiral Damage Multiplier (4 ranks)** — Increases the base damage of Crimson Spiral by 10% per rank. At max rank, Crimson Spiral deals 40% more damage, which also indirectly increases the damage of the Bleed stacks it applies through Hemorrhage.

4. **Bleed Damage Per Tick (3 ranks)** — Increases the damage per second of each Bleed stack by 8% per rank. At max rank, each stack deals 24% more damage per second, which is a massive boost when you have 6-8 stacks active.

5. **Switching Signal Window Extension (2 ranks)** — Extends the tag window after Crimson Spiral by 0.3 seconds per rank. While Bleed does not disable enemies, the Switching Signal window is still important for maintaining the tag combo chain with your other heroes.

Total investment is 19 skill points. The remaining points can go into minor survivability nodes or bleed duration extensions.

### Equipment for Bleed DPS Burst Roxy

Weapon selection for DPS Burst Roxy centers on the Crimson Blade line, which provides passive bonuses to Bleed damage. The Crimson Blade of the Abyss, obtained from the Crimson Hollow dungeon, is the best-in-slot weapon because it increases Bleed damage per tick by 15% and adds a 10% chance to apply Bleed on any hit, independent of the normal threshold. This means Roxy can potentially trigger Bleed in just two basic attacks if the random proc fires.

Armor should prioritize attack power and Bleed enhancement. The Bloodletter's Vest increases the damage of Crimson Spiral by 10%, while the Razor Gauntlets increase critical strike chance on Bleed-related attacks. The accessory slot should hold the Berzerker's Ring, which increases Signal Skill damage based on active ailment count — since Roxy maintains 6-8 Bleed stacks, this ring provides a massive damage boost to Crimson Spiral.

### Familiar Pairing for Bleed DPS Burst Roxy

The Shadow Weasel Familiar is the optimal pairing for DPS Burst Roxy. Its passive ability, "Lingering Wound," extends the duration of each Bleed stack by 15%. This means Bleed stacks last approximately 7 seconds instead of 6 seconds at base, giving Roxy more time to re-apply stacks before they expire. With the Shadow Weasel, maintaining 6-8 Bleed stacks is significantly easier because the re-application window is wider.

The Spider Familiar is an alternative that adds a poison effect alongside Bleed, providing additional DOT damage. While poison does not interact with the tag combo system the way Bleed does, it contributes to total damage output. The Spider is recommended for content where you need maximum raw damage and the crowd-control aspects of the tag combo system are less relevant.

### Bleed DPS Burst Roxy Tag Combo Rotation

Roxy's DPS Burst rotation is designed to maximize Bleed stacks and trigger Crimson Spiral as frequently as possible:

1. Open with three basic attacks (6 hits total) to trigger Bleed → Crimson Spiral fires.

2. During Crimson Spiral, Hemorrhage applies 4-6 additional Bleed stacks.

3. During the Switching Signal window, tag to Dana → Dana applies Freeze → Switching Signal fires.

4. Tag to Lute → Lute applies Knockdown → Switching Signal fires.

5. Tag back to Roxy → three basic attacks → re-trigger Bleed → Crimson Spiral fires again.

6. Maintain 6-8 Bleed stacks throughout the encounter.

## DOT Pressure Build: Sustained Attrition Alternative

The DOT Pressure build trades burst damage for maximum sustained attrition. Instead of trying to rapidly stack Bleed, this build extends Bleed duration and maximizes the damage of each individual stack. The goal is to apply Bleed once, let it tick for an extended period, and use the time while Bleed is active to control the battlefield with tag combos.

### Skill Tree Path for DOT Pressure Roxy

1. **Bleed Duration Extension (5 ranks)** — Extends the base duration of each Bleed stack by 0.8 seconds per rank. At max rank, Bleed lasts 10 seconds instead of 6 seconds, providing a much wider window to maintain stacks.

2. **Hemorrhage (4 ranks)** — Fewer ranks than the DPS Burst build because the priority is duration, not stack velocity. Four ranks still provide a 60% chance per Crimson Spiral hit for additional stacks.

3. **Bleed Damage Per Tick (5 ranks)** — Maximum investment in per-stack damage. At max rank, each stack deals 40% more damage per second.

4. **Rapid Incision (2 ranks)** — Reduces the hit threshold to 4 instead of 6. Less investment than DPS Burst because the DOT Pressure build relies on extended duration, not rapid application.

5. **Bleed Spread (3 ranks)** — When Crimson Spiral triggers while an enemy has Bleed, nearby enemies have a 20% chance per rank to receive one Bleed stack. At max rank, Crimson Spiral can spread Bleed to all nearby enemies, making DOT Pressure excellent for group encounters.

Total investment is 19 skill points. The DOT Pressure build excels in encounters with multiple enemies, where Bleed Spread can create a DOT cascade that damages everything simultaneously.

### Equipment for DOT Pressure Roxy

The Leechstone Ring is the core accessory for DOT Pressure Roxy. It restores 1% of Roxy's maximum health whenever a Bleed tick deals damage. With 6-8 stacks of Bleed on multiple enemies, this provides significant passive sustain, allowing Roxy to stay on the field longer. The Crimson Blade remains a good weapon choice for the raw Bleed damage bonus, and the Bloodletter's Vest provides the Crimson Spiral damage enhancement.

## Roxy's Team Composition Considerations

Roxy works best in team compositions where other heroes provide crowd control while she deals damage. Because Bleed does not disable enemies, Roxy needs her tag-team partners to handle crowd control so she can safely apply and maintain Bleed stacks. The strongest Roxy team is Dana + Lute + Roxy, where Dana provides Freeze, Lute provides Knockdown, and Roxy provides sustained DPS through Bleed.

An alternative team is Castella + Dana + Roxy, which trades Lute's Knockdown for Castella's Airborne. This team has longer crowd-control duration but slightly less tag combo synergy because Castella's Airborne does not interact with Bleed the way Knockdown does. The choice between these compositions depends on which heroes you have invested in.

## FAQ

### Is Roxy better than Lute for DPS?

It depends on the encounter. Lute's DPS Burst build deals higher damage in short encounters where you can trigger Signal Skills rapidly. Roxy's Bleed DPS is stronger in extended encounters because her damage ramps up over time as Bleed stacks accumulate. For boss fights lasting longer than 60 seconds, Roxy typically outdamages Lute. For shorter fights, Lute is more efficient. For a detailed comparison, see our [best builds guide](/en/builds/best-builds-guide/).

### How many Bleed stacks can Roxy maintain simultaneously?

The theoretical maximum is unlimited, but in practice, maintaining more than 8-10 stacks is difficult because early stacks begin expiring before you can re-apply them. With the Shadow Weasel Familiar and the DOT Pressure build's duration extensions, maintaining 8 stacks is realistic. With the DPS Burst build and Hemorrhage, you can briefly reach 10-12 stacks during Crimson Spiral, but some will expire before you can re-trigger.

### Does Bleed work on bosses in Very Hard dungeons?

Yes, Bleed works on all bosses, including Very Hard bosses. Unlike Freeze, which some Very Hard bosses partially resist, Bleed has no resistance mechanic in the current game version. This makes Roxy uniquely valuable in Very Hard content — she is the one DPS hero whose primary mechanic is never diminished by boss resistances. However, some bosses can clear active ailments during phase transitions, which resets your Bleed stacks.

### What Familiar should I pair with Roxy?

The Shadow Weasel is the best Familiar for both Roxy builds because extending Bleed duration benefits both DPS Burst and DOT Pressure. The Spider Familiar is a situational alternative for DOT Pressure when you want additional poison DOT damage. For DPS Burst, the Inferno Drake provides an attack power bonus on Signal Skill triggers, but it is less impactful for Roxy than for Lute because Roxy's damage comes primarily from Bleed ticks rather than raw Signal Skill damage. Check our [Familiar guide](/en/familiars/complete-familiar-guide/) for Familiar locations.

### Can Roxy function without a Stun Lock team?

Roxy can function without crowd control, but she is significantly harder to play. Without Freeze or Knockdown from her tag partners, Roxy must dodge enemy attacks while applying Bleed, which interrupts her attack string and delays Bleed triggers. In solo play or non-Stun Lock teams, the DOT Pressure build is safer than DPS Burst because the longer Bleed duration gives you more flexibility to dodge without losing stacks.
