## How Dodge Windows and i-Frames Work

Understanding the exact moment of invincibility is the first step toward building a reliable Beast of Reincarnation dodge guide strategy. Every dodge in the game has a fixed number of invincibility frames (i-frames) that determine how forgiving your timing can be. The standard dodge roll provides **12 i-frames** at 60 FPS, which translates to roughly 0.2 seconds of invulnerability. However, this window can be extended through specific gear affixes and the **Koo Bond Level**.

The mechanic is further complicated by terrain and enemy hitbox shapes. A horizontal slash from a massive boss might require a perfectly timed forward dodge through the blade, while a vertical slam demands a lateral dash. The direction of your dodge matters because your hurtbox shifts during the animation, and certain attacks are coded to track your position until the very last frame.

| Dodge Type | i-Frames | Stamina Cost | Special Property |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Standard Roll | 12 | 25 | Basic invincibility |
| Quick Step (Dagger) | 10 | 15 | Faster recovery, shorter distance |
| Phased Dash (Koo) | 18 | 40 | Requires Bond Level 3, phases through enemies |
| Perfect Dodge | 12 + 6 Bonus | 25 | Triggers Dodge Advantage, slows time briefly |

The table above illustrates why mixing dodge types is crucial for high-level play. The **Phased Dash**, unlocked through your companion, costs significantly more stamina but allows you to reposition behind an enemy without triggering its tracking AI. Community testing on the official Discord suggests that the Phased Dash also provides a single frame of "ghost" status at the very end, which ignores collision with smaller enemies. According to player experience, combining a Perfect Dodge with a **Phase Shift** from Koo is the primary method for avoiding the final boss's arena-wide devastation attack.

### The Relationship Between Stamina and Evasion

Stamina management dictates the rhythm of combat far more than health does in prolonged fights. Every action, from sprinting to attacking, draws from the same resource pool, but dodging is the most punishing consumer. Running your stamina bar to zero triggers an **Exhaustion State** that locks you out of dodging for 2 seconds—a death sentence in high-tier content.

Veteran players treat stamina as a "green health bar," because losing it often leads directly to losing actual health. The standard recovery delay of 1.2 seconds after a dodge means you cannot spam the roll button and expect to survive. You must time your evasions to allow for a tick of recovery between them.

- The **Stamina Surge** passive reduces recovery delay by 0.3 seconds, which is mandatory for advanced combo guides.
- The **Wind Dancer** armor set grants an additional 5% dodge chance and a unique visual effect when a Perfect Dodge is performed.
- Never let your stamina dip below 30% when a boss is in its "Ultimate Telegraph" animation, because that is when the game expects you to use your best dodge.

## Mastering the Beast of Reincarnation Combo Guide

A true Beast of Reincarnation combo guide focuses on the seamless transition from defense to offense. A combo is not just a string of light and heavy attacks; it is a fluid sequence that uses dodge cancels to bypass recovery frames. Animation canceling is the dividing line between players who clear content and those who get stuck on **The Prisoner** for weeks.

The most fundamental cancel is the **Roll Cancel**, which allows you to interrupt the end of a heavy attack swing with a dodge input. This lets you skip the 0.5-second recovery animation of a charged heavy attack, immediately flowing into a light attack chain. The timing is strict, requiring the dodge input to be buffered right as the damage number pops up on screen. Mastery of this technique effectively increases your damage per second by roughly 30% compared to letting animations play out fully, according to aggregated community data.

The **Greatsword** lacks a quick dodge attack but gains hyper armor during the final frames of a heavy swing, allowing you to trade blows if your dodge stamina is empty. The **Gauntlet** class can chain a dodge directly into a charged uppercut that launches smaller enemies, creating space.

- **Dual Blades**: Forward dodge attacks apply a bleed stack if chained into a full combo.
- **Greatsword**: Dodge sideways into a horizontal cleave to hit multiple targets while avoiding vertical strikes.
- **Tome**: A perfect dodge instantly readies a charged spell, allowing for an immediate high-damage counter.

## Advanced Koo Techniques Guide

The Beast of Reincarnation Koo techniques guide is the most complex part of the combat system because it involves managing a semi-autonomous AI partner. Koo is not just a pet; he is an extension of your own hitbox and a source of utility that can interrupt boss patterns. Properly utilizing dog commands combat mechanics requires binding the "Attack," "Return," and "Phase" commands to easily accessible keys.

The default "Guard" command is a trap in high-end play because it roots Koo in place, making him an easy target for area-of-effect attacks. Instead, the "Phase" command should be your default state, as it makes Koo immune to damage while he repositions. A well-timed **Phase Command** can be used to instantly recall Koo from a dangerous position, which is essential when fighting bosses that apply stacking debuffs to nearby targets.

### Command List and Priority

Koo's AI prioritizes attacking enemies that are about to land a hit on you, which is why he often leaps into cleave damage. You can override this behavior with direct commands. The **Sic 'Em** command makes Koo focus on a single target, and if that target is attacking you, Koo's next hit will be a guaranteed critical strike. This synergy is explored in our [advanced builds and upgrades guide](/en/combat/beast-of-reincarnation-best-combat-upgrades/) for a deeper dive into maximizing your damage output.

- **Phase Command**: Order Koo to teleport to your location and become intangible for 2 seconds. Use this to rescue him from boss ultimates.
- **Sic 'Em**: Koo's next attack applies a **Weaken** debuff, reducing target damage by 20% for 5 seconds.
- **Fetch**: A utility command that can interrupt the boss's **Soul Drain** channeling ability by making Koo steal a critical item.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I consistently trigger a Perfect Dodge?**

The key is to watch the enemy's weapon trail, not their body movement. Input the dodge command exactly when the glow on the weapon reaches its brightest point, which indicates the active damage frames are about to begin. This timing is more reliable than reacting to the boss's wind-up animation.

**Why does my Koo keep dying instantly in boss fights?**

Koo takes massive damage if he is hit during his own attack animation. Use the **Phase Command** preemptively when you see a boss winding up a large area-of-effect attack, rather than waiting for him to be hit. The command makes him intangible and teleports him to your position instantly.

**What is the best weapon for a dodge-heavy playstyle?**

The **Dual Blades** are widely considered the strongest for evasion builds because their dodge attack has inherent i-frames and a built-in lunge. This allows you to chain dodges directly into offensive pressure without the brief vulnerability window that other weapons have on their dodge attacks.

**How do I respec my combat upgrades if I invested poorly?**

You can respec your combat traits by using a **Tear of Atonement** at any campfire, but this item is rare. Before doing so, visit the training dummy in the Hub area to test new builds without spending resources. The game saves your active build separately from your training build.

**Is poise or mobility better for late-game bosses?**

Mobility is universally better because late-game bosses apply debuffs on hit that ignore poise. Being able to avoid hits entirely via this Beast of Reincarnation dodge guide methods is superior to trying to absorb them, as late-game enemies deal shadow damage that bypasses a percentage of armor on every hit.
