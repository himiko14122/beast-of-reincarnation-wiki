## Breakdown of the Latest Beast of Reincarnation Patch Notes

The most recent set of **Beast of Reincarnation patch notes** delivered sweeping balance adjustments, particularly targeting weapon scaling and enemy aggression in the **Blighted Forest** region. Developers explicitly acknowledged that the **Koo** companion was underperforming in spectral combat, leading to a significant buff to his **Spectral Lunge** ability. This attack now scales more aggressively with the player's **Spirit Resonance** stat, and the enhanced tracking eliminates the whiffing issues that plagued previous versions. Meanwhile, the **Emma Iai-Jutsu** stance has had its parry window adjusted by two frames, a change that rewards precision timing over passive blocking and significantly raises the skill ceiling for melee-focused players. The days of simply holding block are gone, as bosses like the **Corrupted Samurai** now possess guard-breaking combos that force players to master the dodge mechanic.
