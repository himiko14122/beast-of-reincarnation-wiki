### The Fusion Economy Shift

The biggest shift in the **Beast of Reincarnation version history** was the transition from capturing rare spawns to the **Beast Fusion Lab** in version 1.2.0, a change that dramatically altered the in-game economy. Instead of grinding specific coordinate locations for rare beasts, players now had to hoard essence from common captures to synthesize rare ones. This mechanic rewards patience and market speculation, which is why veteran merchants often stockpile specific elemental essences ahead of anticipated buffs in the next major patch.
