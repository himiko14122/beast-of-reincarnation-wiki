## Understanding the Freeze Status Ailment and Frost Gauge

Freeze is one of the most potent Status Ailments in DragonSword: Awakening, capable of completely immobilizing enemies and setting up devastating extended combo chains. Unlike Knockdown or Shock, which primarily interrupt enemy actions, Freeze locks a target in a solid ice block, rendering them helpless for a fixed duration. This opens a critical window for high-damage follow-ups, particularly when transitioning into Airborne chains via Switching Signal techniques.

The Freeze Status Ailment is applied through a hidden Frost Gauge system. Each enemy has an invisible meter that fills when they take cryo-aligned damage or are affected by freeze-inflicting skills. Once the gauge is full, the enemy enters the Frozen state. The base duration of Freeze is four seconds, but this can be extended through specific hero passives, Familiar buffs, or gear attributes. During Freeze, the enemy cannot move, attack, or call for reinforcements, making it a superior lockdown tool compared to Shock, which only slows actions.

Key interactions with the Frost Gauge include:
- **Cryo damage accumulation:** Heroes with ice-element attacks, such as Dana and Chako, fill the gauge fastest.

- **Environmental hazards:** Certain zones on the Continent of Orbis feature freezing winds or ice geysers that passively build the Frost Gauge on both players and enemies.

- **Resistance and immunity:** Boss-tier enemies and Abyssal Direwolves have partial resistance, requiring roughly 30% more gauge fill to freeze. Some raid bosses are completely immune, so check the Hero Request details before committing to a freeze composition.

The Freeze ailment also synergizes uniquely with other Status Ailments. A Frozen enemy hit by a heavy blunt attack (like Johnny’s charged smash) will shatter the ice, dealing bonus shatter damage. However, this removes the Freeze state early, so weigh the trade-off between burst damage and extended crowd control.

## Dana: The Frostbringer’s Kit and Freeze Application

Dana is the premier hero for initiating Freeze combos, thanks to her innate cryo affinity and skills designed to rapidly fill the Frost Gauge. Her basic attacks have a small cryo component, but her Signal Skills are where she shines.

**Signal Skill – Glacial Lance:** Dana hurls a spear of ice that deals moderate damage and applies a massive chunk of Frost Gauge fill. A direct hit on a non-boss enemy often results in an instant Freeze if no resistance is present. This skill has a 12-second cooldown, making it a reliable opener.

**Switching Signal – Blizzard Field:** When tagged in via Switching Signal, Dana creates a persistent AoE snowstorm around her for six seconds. Enemies standing in the field take continuous cryo damage, rapidly building the Frost Gauge. This is the core of many freeze combo chains, as it allows other heroes to contribute while Dana is off-field.

**Passive – Cryomancy:** Dana’s critical hits reduce the cooldown of Glacial Lance by one second. This passive encourages a high-crit build to maximize freeze uptime. In optimal gear, Dana can freeze-lock non-resistant enemies indefinitely by cycling Glacial Lance and Blizzard Field.

Dana’s freeze application is so potent that she often becomes the anchor of any cryo-based team. According to community reports, a fully built Dana with 70%+ crit rate can maintain near-permanent freeze on elite mobs in the Rift System, trivializing high-difficulty floors.

## Chako: The Cryo Amplifier and Sub-DPS

Chako’s role in freeze combo chains is that of an amplifier and secondary freeze applier. While she cannot apply Freeze as quickly as Dana, her kit is designed to exploit frozen targets and extend the duration of the ailment.

**Signal Skill – Frostbite Slash:** A quick three-hit combo that deals bonus damage to Frozen enemies. Each hit also re-applies a small amount of Frost Gauge fill, effectively refreshing the freeze duration if the target is already frozen. This allows Chako to keep a boss locked down almost as well as Dana.

**Switching Signal – Cryo Prison:** Chako summons an ice cage around the target, dealing damage and leaving a chilling debuff that slows enemy movement and increases Frost Gauge accumulation by 20% for eight seconds. Tag in Dana after this debuff to see her Glacial Lance fill nearly half the gauge in one hit.

**Passive – Frostbite:** Chako’s attacks against Frozen enemies ignore 15% of their defense. This makes her the ideal follow-up after Dana’s initial freeze, as she can shred through frozen health bars with ease.

The synergy between Dana and Chako is undeniable. A common rotation is: Dana tags out after Blizzard Field, Chako tags in with Cryo Prison to amplify gauge gain, then Dana is switched back via Switching Signal to reapply Glacial Lance and re-freeze the target. This loop, often called the "Frozen Waltz," is a staple in advanced tag combos.

## Freeze to Airborne Extended Chains

One of the most devastating applications of Freeze is transitioning into an Airborne chain. The Freeze state holds enemies perfectly still, making it trivial to launch them with an Airborne-initiating skill. This allows teams to stack massive air combo multipliers before sending the enemy crashing down.

| Chain | Heroes | Effect | Notes |
|-------|--------|--------|-------|
| Frozen Waltz | Dana → Chako → Dana | Freeze → amplify gauge → re-freeze | Chako Cryo Prison +20% gauge gain for 8s |
| Freeze → Airborne launch | Dana → Kalien | Freeze → Rising Dragon Airborne | Launch cannot be dodged while Frozen |
| Freeze → Airborne extended | Dana → Chako → Kalien → Dana | Freeze → Cryo Prison → Airborne → Blizzard mid-air | Can exceed 50 hits, 200%+ combo multiplier |
| Shatter cycle | Dana → Veronica → Johnny | Freeze → defense shred → charged slam | Shatter at last moment for max lockdown |

The basic sequence is:
1. **Freeze the target** using Dana’s Glacial Lance or Chako’s amplified Frost Gauge.

2. **Launch with Airborne Signal:** Switch to a hero with a launching Switching Signal, such as Kalien’s Rising Dragon or Aria’s Cyclone Kick. Because the enemy is Frozen, the launch cannot be dodged or blocked.

3. **Air juggle:** Continue the air combo using any hero’s aerial attacks. The Freeze state will typically expire mid-air, but the enemy is already locked into the juggle state.

4. **Slam finisher:** End with a slam attack from a heavy hero like Johnny or Logan to trigger shatter damage if the Freeze is still active, or simply maximize fall damage.

A sample advanced chain using Dana, Chako, and Kalien:
- **Dana** uses Glacial Lance to freeze.

- **Dana** tags out, **Chako** tags in with Cryo Prison (amplifies gauge, deals damage).

- **Chako** uses Frostbite Slash to refresh freeze, then tags out.

- **Kalien** tags in with Rising Dragon (Airborne launch).

- **Kalien** performs two air slashes, then tags out.

- **Dana** tags back in with Blizzard Field (adds cryo damage mid-air).

- **Kalien** finishes with a slam.

This chain can easily exceed 50 hits on a single target, building the combo multiplier to over 200% bonus damage. In the Rift System, where enemies have bloated health pools, such chains are essential for time-efficient clears.

## Heroes That Synergize with Freeze

Beyond Dana and Chako, several other heroes have kits that either benefit from or contribute to Freeze combos:

| Position | Hero | Role |
|----------|------|------|
| Ranged Freeze finish | Roxy | Icy Volley fills remaining Frost Gauge from distance, Frost Arrows passive adds 10% slow on crit |
| Elemental buffer | Theresia | Encouragement skill boosts all elemental damage by 25% including cryo |
| Familiar support | Ornette | Valiant Hatchling specced cryo provides passive Frost Gauge accumulation |
| Post-shatter nuke | Veronica | Defense shred stacks multiplicatively with Chako's Frostbite passive for devastating single-target nuke |

- **Roxy:** Her Signal Skill, Icy Volley, fires a spread of cryo arrows. While not as potent as Dana’s lance, it can finish filling the Frost Gauge from a safe distance. Her passive, Frost Arrows, adds a 10% slow on critical hits, which indirectly helps set up freezes.

- **Theresia:** As a support hero, Theresia’s Encouragement skill boosts all elemental damage by 25% for the party. This includes cryo damage, making Dana and Chako’s gauge fill significantly faster.

- **Ornette:** Her Familiar, Valiant Hatchling, can be specced into a cryo build, providing passive Frost Gauge accumulation over time. Combined with Dana’s Blizzard Field, this creates an inescapable freezing zone.

- **Veronica:** A less obvious choice, but her Signal Skill inflicts a defense shred that stacks multiplicatively with Chako’s Frostbite passive. After a freeze chain, Veronica can tag in for a devastating single-target nuke on the shattered target.

Avoid pairing Freeze with heroes who apply Shock or Knockdown too aggressively, as these can break the Freeze state prematurely if not timed correctly. The Red Fox Mercenaries, for instance, often use fire-based attacks that can thaw frozen enemies.

## Building a Cryo-Focused Team

To maximize freeze combo efficiency, your team composition and gear should focus on cryo synergy. Here’s a recommended setup:

**Core Team:**
- **Dana** (Main DPS/Freeze Initiator)

- **Chako** (Sub-DPS/Freeze Extender)

- **Theresia** (Support/Elemental Buffer)

- **Ornette** (Familiar Support/Cryo Familiar)

**Gear Priorities:**
- **Dana:** Maximize crit rate and cryo damage. The “Frozen Heart” artifact set increases Frost Gauge fill by 40%, making it mandatory. Weapon should be an ice-enchanted spear.

- **Chako:** Build attack speed and crit damage. Faster attacks mean more Frostbite Slash procs. The “Glacier’s Edge” dual blades have a passive that extends freeze duration by one second.

- **Theresia:** Standard support build with high energy recharge to keep Encouragement active.

- **Ornette:** Equip the Valiant Hatchling with a “Frost Breath” skill book. This Familiar can maintain a low but steady Frost Gauge fill, acting as a passive freeze support.

**Alternate Options:**
If you lack Chako, consider pairing Dana with **Eileen** for AoE freeze potential. Eileen’s wide-range ice magic can freeze multiple mobs, allowing Dana to focus on elite targets. For boss fights, replace Ornette with **Lute** for his damage amplification on debuffed enemies.

In the Nameless Soul artifact system, prioritize those with “Status Ailment Duration” and “Cryo Penetration” stats. The “Soul of the Frozen Wastes” is a legendary Nameless Soul that increases all freeze duration by 1.5 seconds—an absolute game-changer.

## Advanced Freeze Tactics in the Rift System

The Rift System in DragonSword: Awakening tests your mastery of Status Ailments. Floors 50+ feature enemies with high ailment resistance, making freeze chains more challenging but even more rewarding.

**Tactic 1: Pre-Freeze Layering**
Before engaging a pack, have Ornette’s Familiar use Frost Breath to start building the gauge. As enemies approach, Dana opens with Blizzard Field. This overlapping cryo damage often freezes the entire pack within two seconds.

**Tactic 2: Freeze Peeling**
In co-op or with NPC allies, designate one hero (usually Dana) as the “freezer” and others as “peelers.” The freezer locks down the priority target while peelers handle adds. This is essential in the Organa Order missions where multiple Abyssal Direwolves spawn.

**Tactic 3: Shatter Cycles**
On bosses that can be frozen, do not immediately shatter. Instead, use the freeze window to apply debuffs (Veronica’s defense shred, Chako’s Cryo Prison). Just before freeze expires, have Johnny tag in for a charged slam to trigger shatter damage at the last possible moment, maximizing the lockdown period.

According to estimated data from high-floor Rift runners, a well-executed freeze chain can increase damage output by 40% compared to un-optimized play. The key is knowing when to extend the freeze and when to shatter for burst.

## Freeze Interactions with Other Status Ailments

Freeze does not exist in a vacuum. Its interactions with other Status Ailments can make or break a combo:

- **Freeze + Airborne:** As discussed, the most synergistic pairing. Frozen enemies cannot resist launch.

- **Freeze + Shock:** Do not overlap. Shock’s slow is redundant on a Frozen target, and some Shock skills can break the ice block early without triggering shatter damage.

- **Freeze + Knockdown:** A target cannot be knocked down while Frozen. However, if a Knockdown is applied just as freeze breaks, it can extend the enemy’s helplessness. Timing is crucial.

- **Freeze + Bleed:** Bleed ticks continue while the enemy is Frozen. This is a potent DoT combination, especially with heroes like Aria who can apply both Bleed and Airborne.

## FAQ

### What is the maximum freeze duration in DragonSword: Awakening?

The base freeze duration is four seconds. With Dana’s passive, Chako’s Cryo Prison extension, and the “Soul of the Frozen Wastes” Nameless Soul, you can achieve up to 8.5 seconds of continuous freeze on a single target. This requires precise timing and high gear investment.

### Can bosses be frozen?

Most field bosses and some Rift bosses can be frozen, but they have increased resistance. Raid bosses and weekly world bosses from the Hundred Soul event are typically immune. Check the Hero Request panel for boss immunities before engaging.

### Which hero is better for freeze: Dana or Chako?

Dana is the superior freeze initiator due to her Glacial Lance and Blizzard Field. Chako excels at extending and exploiting freezes. They are best used together rather than compared directly.

### Does freeze work on the Abyssal Direwolf?

The Abyssal Direwolf has high cryo resistance, requiring roughly 50% more Frost Gauge fill. It can be frozen, but the duration is halved. Use Chako’s Cryo Prison to amplify gauge gain, and consider using Theresia’s buff to overcome the resistance.

### How do I counter enemies that use freeze against me?

Equip gear with “Status Ailment Resistance” or use a Familiar with the “Purifying Flame” skill. Hero Theresia’s Encouragement also provides a brief ailment immunity window. In PvP, dodging Dana’s Glacial Lance is critical, as it is a slow projectile.

### Can I build a full cryo team without Dana?

Yes, though it is less optimal. A team of Chako, Eileen, Theresia, and Ornette with cryo Familiars can still maintain respectable freeze uptime. Focus on high attack speed to compensate for the lack of Dana’s burst gauge fill.

### What is the best artifact set for a freeze-focused Dana?

The “Frozen Heart” set is mandatory for the 40% Frost Gauge fill bonus. Pair it with the “Ice Witch’s Pendant” accessory to increase shatter damage by 30%. For Nameless Souls, seek any that boost cryo damage or Status Ailment duration.

### Is freeze viable in the current meta?

Absolutely. Freeze chains are considered top-tier for Rift System progression and Hero Request missions. In the latest update from Hound13, Dana received a minor buff to her Blizzard Field duration, solidifying her place in the meta. Community tier lists consistently rank cryo teams in the A+ tier for PvE content.

### How does the Frost Gauge interact with Familiar attacks?

Familiars with cryo skills contribute to the Frost Gauge independently. The Valiant Hatchling’s Frost Breath adds a small, continuous amount. Multiple Familiars with cryo skills can stack, but there is a diminishing return after two cryo Familiars.

### What is the best F2P freeze team?

For free-to-play players, a team of Dana (obtainable from the story), Chako (early game unlock), Lute (starter hero for damage amplification), and a cryo-specced Familiar is highly effective. Focus resources on Dana’s crit rate and Chako’s attack speed.
