Dana, the Ice Mage of the Organa Order, stands as one of the most tactically demanding heroes in DragonSword: Awakening. Unlike straightforward damage dealers such as Lute or Johnny, Dana operates through a layered system of frost accumulation, battlefield control, and a unique companion mechanic—the Chako familiar. This guide dissects every aspect of Dana's kit, from basic frost application to advanced Freeze chaining, providing the precise data you need to master her cryomantic arts on the Continent of Orbis.

## Understanding Dana's Core Frost Mechanics

Dana’s entire playstyle revolves around the Frost Gauge, a resource that builds on enemies through her attacks and abilities. When the Frost Gauge reaches its maximum threshold of 100 points, the target suffers the Freeze Status Ailment, becoming immobilized for 3 seconds and taking bonus shatter damage from subsequent physical attacks. This mechanic makes Dana an unparalleled crowd control specialist, but it requires precise management to maximize her effectiveness.

The Frost Gauge decays at a rate of 5 points per second when no frost damage is applied, so consistent pressure is essential. Dana’s basic attacks apply 15 Frost Gauge per hit, while her skills scale this further. Her Signal Skill, Frost Lance, applies 30 Frost Gauge instantly and pierces through enemies in a line, making it invaluable for controlling clustered mobs. Her Switching Signal, Glacial Spike, consumes the Frost Gauge to deal massive damage based on the amount of frost accumulated, which resets the gauge afterward. This creates a fundamental decision tree: freeze enemies for prolonged control, or detonate the gauge for burst damage.

### The Chako Familiar: Frost Stone Golem Mechanics

Dana’s defining feature is Chako, a Frost Stone Golem familiar that acts as an autonomous turret and damage amplifier. Chako deploys automatically when Dana enters combat, hovering near her and firing frost bolts at the nearest enemy every 2 seconds. Each bolt applies 10 Frost Gauge, which stacks with Dana’s own applications. However, Chako’s true power lies in its interaction with the Rift System.

When Dana activates her Hero Request ability, Chako enters an overcharged state for 12 seconds. During this window, its attack speed doubles, and its frost bolts apply 20 Frost Gauge per hit. Additionally, any enemy affected by Freeze within Chako’s range triggers an AoE frost explosion dealing 150% of Dana’s attack power as damage. This synergy allows Dana to freeze one target and then chain-explode groups, making her devastating in both single-target and multi-target scenarios. According to community reports, a well-timed Hero Request can clear entire waves in the Abyssal Direwolf dungeon with minimal gear.

## Optimal Skill Build and Rotation

Dana’s skill priority should focus on maximizing Frost Gauge accumulation and Chako’s uptime. The recommended skill order for leveling is: Frost Lance (Signal Skill) > Chako Enhancement > Glacial Spike (Switching Signal) > Permafrost Aura. This order ensures you have consistent frost application tools before investing in burst or passive benefits.

Frost Lance at maximum rank reduces its cooldown to 6 seconds and increases its Frost Gauge application to 45 points per cast. This becomes your primary tool for initiating Freeze on priority targets. Chako Enhancement extends the Hero Request duration by 3 seconds and increases the overcharged attack speed bonus by 25%. Glacial Spike should be used as a finisher when an enemy is near death or when you need to quickly eliminate a high-threat target, as it benefits from the full Frost Gauge consumption. Permafrost Aura provides a passive 10% chance to apply 5 Frost Gauge to all enemies within a small radius every second, which synergizes with Chako’s constant damage.

In combat, your rotation should follow this pattern: Engage with basic attacks to start building Frost Gauge, use Frost Lance to spike the gauge toward 100, activate Hero Request when Chako is positioned near multiple enemies, then unleash Glacial Spike on the primary target if the situation demands burst. Never waste Glacial Spike on a low-Frost Gauge target unless it secures a kill, as the damage difference between a 20-gauge and 90-gauge detonation is nearly 300% higher.

## Best Team Synergy with Aria and Castella

Dana thrives in compositions that capitalize on Freeze and Status Ailment synergies. The premier team setup for Dana includes Aria as a secondary mage and Castella as a support. Aria’s fire-based attacks can trigger Melt reactions on frozen targets, dealing 50% bonus damage and clearing the Freeze status but leaving a Defense Reduction debuff. This trade-off is excellent for boss fights where sustained damage matters more than control.

Castella provides the critical role of energy regeneration and healing, ensuring Dana can use her Hero Request more frequently. Castella’s passive, Organa’s Blessing, reduces cooldowns by 1 second every time a Status Ailment is applied, which perfectly complements Dana’s constant Freeze application. With this trio, you can maintain near-permanent Freeze on bosses while cycling through high-damage windows. An alternative setup replaces Aria with Kalsion for physical shatter damage, as his Charged Strike deals 200% damage to frozen targets.

For a more defensive approach, pairing Dana with Theresia provides a frontline that can group enemies with her taunt, allowing Chako’s AoE explosions to hit maximum targets. Theresia’s shield also prevents interruptions during Dana’s Hero Request channel, which is crucial in high-level Rift System content where a single hit can cancel the activation.

### Tag Combos and Status Ailment Chains

Dana’s Freeze is a Tier-1 Status Ailment in the game’s hierarchy, meaning it can be overwritten by higher-tier ailments like Shock but not by lower ones like Bleed. This makes her pairing with heroes like Roxy or Logan tricky, as their Bleed applications will not clear Freeze but will not benefit from the shatter bonus either. For optimal Tag Combos, you want heroes who can exploit the frozen state without removing it.

The ideal Tag Combo sequence is: Dana applies Freeze → Kalsion uses Charged Strike for shatter damage → Johnny follows with a basic combo to build his own gauge while the target is still recovering. This chain works because shatter damage does not remove Freeze; only Melt reactions (fire attacks) or Glacial Spike consumption do. If you have Castella in the team, her Switching Signal can extend the Freeze duration by 1.5 seconds, giving your physical damage dealers more time to capitalize.

## Gear and Stat Priority

Dana scales primarily with Magic Attack and Cooldown Reduction. Her gear should focus on the Frostbite Set, which increases Frost Gauge accumulation by 20% at 4 pieces and adds a 10% chance to apply an additional 10 Frost Gauge at 6 pieces. The weapon slot should prioritize the Staff of Eternal Winter, which extends Freeze duration by 1 second and increases shatter damage by 30%.

Secondary stats should emphasize Attack Speed to apply more basic attacks (and thus more Frost Gauge) and Energy Recharge to cycle Hero Request faster. Critical Hit Rate and Critical Damage are lower priorities because Dana’s primary value comes from control and Chako’s consistent output, not her personal burst. However, if you are running the shatter-focused build with Glacial Spike detonations, Critical Damage becomes significantly more valuable, as the spike damage can crit for enormous numbers.

Runes should include Frost Touch for increased Frost Gauge application, Companion’s Might for Chako’s damage, and Time Dilation for cooldown reduction during Hero Request. According to estimated data from high-level players, a fully optimized Dana can maintain Freeze on a boss for 70% of the fight duration, reducing incoming damage by an effective 40% compared to a no-control composition.

## Leveling and Progression Tips

Early-game Dana can feel underwhelming because Chako’s base attack speed is slow, and her Frost Gauge application without skill upgrades is mediocre. Focus on completing Hero Request missions to unlock Chako Enhancement as soon as possible, as this skill transforms her clearing speed. The Valiant Hatchling dungeon is ideal for leveling Dana because the grouped enemies allow Chako’s AoE explosions to chain, and the boss is vulnerable to Freeze.

In the mid-game, prioritize acquiring the Nameless Soul currency to purchase Dana’s exclusive weapon from the Red Fox Mercenaries faction vendor. This weapon increases Chako’s attack range by 30%, allowing it to cover more of the battlefield. For late-game Rift System pushes, Dana requires maximum investment in her Hero Request uptime, as the later floors feature enemies immune to Freeze but not to the raw damage from Chako’s overcharged state.

## Dana in the Current Meta

In the current DragonSword: Awakening meta, Dana occupies a unique niche as the premier control mage. She is not a top-tier damage dealer compared to heroes like Lute or Johnny, but her ability to lock down entire encounters makes her irreplaceable in high-difficulty content. The Organa Order faction synergies further boost her utility, as having three Organa members in a team grants 15% increased Status Ailment duration.

Dana’s main weakness is her reliance on Chako’s positioning; if enemies move out of range, her Frost Gauge application plummets. Maps with highly mobile bosses like the Abyssal Direwolf require precise timing and prediction to maximize Chako’s uptime. Additionally, some late-game bosses possess Frost Resistance, reducing Frost Gauge accumulation by 50%, which significantly hampers her control. In these cases, switching to a pure shatter build with Glacial Spike as the primary damage source is recommended.

## FAQ

### What is the best way to use Dana’s Hero Request in boss fights?

Activate Hero Request when the boss is about to enter a stationary phase or when Chako is positioned directly behind the boss. This maximizes the AoE explosion damage from frozen targets and ensures Chako’s overcharged bolts hit consistently. Time your activation right after applying Freeze for an immediate explosion chain.

### How does Chako’s damage scale with Dana’s stats?

Chako inherits 80% of Dana’s Magic Attack and 50% of her Attack Speed. Cooldown Reduction does not affect Chako’s base attack interval but does reduce the cooldown of Hero Request. Companion-specific runes and gear bonuses apply multiplicatively, so a dedicated Chako build can out-damage Dana herself in sustained fights.

### Can Dana be used as a main DPS instead of a control mage?

Yes, by prioritizing Glacial Spike detonations and Critical Damage, Dana can serve as a burst mage. However, this build is highly gear-dependent and requires the Staff of Eternal Winter plus critical-focused runes. Even then, her sustained damage will not match dedicated DPS heroes like Johnny, but her burst windows can rival them.

### What is the Frost Gauge decay rate, and how can I counteract it?

The Frost Gauge decays at 5 points per second after 2 seconds of no frost application. To counteract this, always weave basic attacks between skills, and position Chako to maintain constant pressure. The Permafrost Aura passive helps mitigate decay, and Castella’s Switching Signal can pause decay for 2 seconds.

### Is Dana viable in PvP content?

Dana is a high-risk, high-reward pick in PvP due to Freeze’s potency against players. A single Freeze can set up a kill combo, but skilled opponents will prioritize killing Chako first, which disables her familiar for 20 seconds. Use Dana in teams with protectors like Theresia or assassins like Renia who can capitalize on frozen targets instantly.

### How do I unlock Chako’s exclusive skins and upgrades?

Chako’s skins are available through the Hundred Soul event shop and the Nameless Soul exchange. Upgrades for Chako’s core stats are tied to Dana’s ascension level; at Ascension 5, Chako gains a permanent 15% Frost Gauge bonus and a visual upgrade to its crystal core.

### What should I do if enemies are immune to Freeze?

Against Freeze-immune enemies, pivot to a raw damage build focusing on Glacial Spike’s base damage and Chako’s overcharged state. The Frost Gauge will not accumulate, but Glacial Spike still deals damage based on your Magic Attack. Pair with Kalsion or Olette for physical damage to compensate for Dana’s reduced control utility.
