## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best way to start the Emma outcast storyline?**

To trigger the **Beast of Reincarnation outcast Emma story**, you must fail the first defense of the Hamlet intentionally. When the boss breaches the gate, do not attack immediately; let Emma take a hit. This triggers her "Soul Fracture" affliction, which begins her memory recovery arc for the rest of the run.

**How do I keep my allies from leaving the party?**

According to the **Beast of Reincarnation NPC characters guide**, loyalty is maintained by triggering their specific affliction events. For example, Gareth requires you to enter the Crimson Arena within 5 minutes of his Rage triggering. Ignoring these timed events, which are often only hinted at in the environment, is the primary reason allies abandon the player.

**Are afflictions really necessary to finish the game?**

While you can reach the credits by curing every affliction, the true ending requires at least 3 allies to be suffering from a Tier 3 affliction simultaneously. The **Beast of Reincarnation Emma affliction guide** is essential because Emma must be one of those three, and she can only reach Tier 3 if she survives the Hamlet siege with her memories intact.

**Why does the lore seem so fragmented?**

The developers intentionally scattered the **Beast of Reincarnation character lore** across item descriptions and missable dialogue to encourage community collaboration. Afflictions like "Memory Rot" actually blur the text in your UI, hiding the lore unless you read the game files or share discoveries with other players, a mechanic praised for its immersion but criticized for its opacity.

**Can I experience all the lore in one playthrough?**

No. The conflicting nature of the ally quests makes it impossible. To see the full **Beast of Reincarnation ally characters** arcs, you must do multiple runs. Saving Lyra requires you to ignore the Silent Bard, and saving the Silent Bard requires you to betray Lyra. The game is designed for a minimum of four complete loops to see every piece of dialogue.
