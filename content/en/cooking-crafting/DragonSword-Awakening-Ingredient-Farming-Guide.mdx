Gathering cooking ingredients in DragonSword: Awakening is a core activity that connects the open-world exploration system to the cooking and crafting progression. Rare ingredients like Frostpeak Truffles and Volcanic Chili are required for the most powerful stat-boosting meals, but they only appear in specific regions and often under specific conditions. Without efficient farming routes, you can spend hours wandering the Continent of Orbis looking for materials that are concentrated in small, easily missed areas. This guide provides optimized farming routes for every ingredient type, from the most common herbs to the rarest DragonSword Spice.

For the fundamentals of what each ingredient is used for, see our [cooking guide](/en/cooking-crafting/cooking-guide/). This guide focuses specifically on where and how to farm ingredients efficiently.

## Ingredient Spawning Mechanics: How the System Works

Before diving into specific routes, it is important to understand how ingredient spawning works in DragonSword: Awakening. Ingredients spawn at fixed gathering nodes throughout the open world. Each node has a respawn timer that determines how long you must wait after harvesting before the node regenerates. Common ingredient nodes respawn in 5-10 minutes, uncommon nodes respawn in 15-20 minutes, and rare nodes respawn in 30-60 minutes.

Ingredient nodes also have visibility conditions. Some nodes only appear during specific times of day — for example, Frostpeak Truffles are easier to spot during the game's nighttime cycle when their bioluminescent glow is visible against the dark ice. Other nodes only appear during specific weather conditions, such as Deep Sea Kelp which is more abundant during storm conditions when water currents stir up additional growth.

Understanding these mechanics means you can plan your farming routes around optimal conditions. If you need Frostpeak Truffles, farm the Glacial Rift at night. If you need Deep Sea Kelp, check the weather conditions before heading to the underwater area. For more on the day-night and weather systems, see our [exploration guide](/en/exploration/continent-of-orbis-exploration-guide/).

## Common Ingredient Farming Routes

Common ingredients are abundant and do not require optimized routes, but knowing where to find them in the highest density saves time when you need large quantities for bulk cooking.

### Orbis Herbs: Field Loop

Orbis Herbs are the most common ingredient in the game, but you need them in large quantities for cooking proficiency leveling. The densest herb clusters are found in the meadow regions south of the Red Fox base camp. A circular loop through this area takes approximately 8 minutes and yields 25-35 Orbis Herbs from 12-15 gathering nodes. The nodes respawn by the time you complete the loop, making this an infinitely repeatable farming route.

### Wild Game Meat: Hunting Path

Wild Game Meat drops from wildlife enemies throughout the open world. The most efficient hunting path follows the forest edge northeast of the base camp, where deer and boar spawn densely. Each kill yields 1-2 Wild Game Meat. A 10-minute hunting session through this path yields 15-25 Wild Game Meat. Using a hero with fast attack speed like Roxy makes hunting more efficient because each kill takes less time.

### River Fish: Fishing Spot Rotation

River Fish are caught at designated fishing spots along rivers and lakes. The best fishing spots are located along the main river that runs through the central valley. A rotation through 6 fishing spots takes approximately 12 minutes and yields 18-24 River Fish. Fishing is a quiet activity that also serves as a break from combat-heavy farming, making it a good activity to alternate with dungeon runs.

### Mountain Salt: Mining Circuit

Mountain Salt deposits are found in the mountainous regions north of the Glacial Rift. A circuit through 8 mining nodes takes approximately 10 minutes and yields 16-24 Mountain Salt. The mining nodes are spread along cliff edges and cave entrances, requiring some vertical navigation. The Stoneback Turtle Familiar, if available, can help locate hidden deposits through its passive detection ability.

## Uncommon Ingredient Farming Routes

Uncommon ingredients require targeted farming in specific regions. Each route below is optimized for a single uncommon ingredient type.

### Frostpeak Truffle: Glacial Rift Night Route

Frostpeak Truffles grow near ice formations in the Glacial Rift region. They are most visible during the nighttime cycle, when their blue bioluminescence makes them easy to spot against the dark ice. The optimal farming route follows the frozen river from the northern entrance, checking ice cave entrances and glacier base areas where truffles grow.

- **Best time**: Night cycle (approximately 20 minutes of game time per cycle)
- **Route duration**: 20 minutes per pass
- **Yield**: 6-10 Frostpeak Truffles per pass
- **Respawn timer**: 15-20 minutes per node
- **Tips**: Equip the Frost Wisp Familiar for passive Frost Gauge charging while gathering, so you can seamlessly transition to combat if ice-element enemies appear.

The Frostpeak Truffle farming route is also an excellent source of Glacial Crystals for crafting, making it a dual-purpose farming activity. For more on the crafting materials obtained here, see our [crafting materials guide](/en/cooking-crafting/crafting-materials-guide/).

### Volcanic Chili: Lava Flow Path

Volcanic Chili grows near lava flows in the volcanic region. Unlike Frostpeak Truffles, Volcanic Chili is visible during any time of day because of its bright red coloration against the dark volcanic rock. The optimal farming route follows the main lava flow from the western entrance, checking the cooled lava formations where Chili plants grow.

- **Best time**: Any time of day
- **Route duration**: 25 minutes per pass
- **Yield**: 4-6 Volcanic Chili per pass
- **Respawn timer**: 20 minutes per node
- **Tips**: Equip a meal with damage reduction (Ocean's Embrace recommended) to mitigate environmental lava tick damage. The damage is minor but accumulates over a long farming session.

Volcanic Chili farming also yields Volcanic Ash from fire-element enemies along the path, making this another dual-purpose route.

### Deep Sea Kelp: Underwater Garden Route

Deep Sea Kelp is harvested from underwater gathering nodes in the deep sea exploration area. The kelp grows in underwater gardens near coral formations. The optimal farming route follows the coral garden loop at medium depth, checking each formation for harvestable kelp.

- **Best time**: Storm weather increases kelp abundance by approximately 30%
- **Route duration**: 35 minutes per pass (underwater movement is slower)
- **Yield**: 5-8 Deep Sea Kelp per pass
- **Respawn timer**: 20-25 minutes per node
- **Tips**: The Abyssal Direwolf Familiar provides a small swimming speed bonus underwater if you obtained it through the August 2026 purchase bonus. Without it, the deep sea route is simply slower.

This area also provides Deep Sea Pearls for crafting, making it the most productive single region for tank-build resources. For more on the deep sea area, see our [deep sea exploration guide](/en/exploration/deep-sea-exploration-guide/).

### Skypeak Honey: High Altitude Collection

Skypeak Honey is collected from beehives at high-altitude locations in the mountain regions. These beehives are positioned on cliff faces and require careful navigation to reach. The optimal route follows the ridge line from the Skypeak Ruins entrance, checking each cliff overhang for hives.

- **Best time**: Day cycle (bees are less aggressive during daytime)
- **Route duration**: 15 minutes per pass
- **Yield**: 3-5 Skypeak Honey per pass
- **Respawn timer**: 20 minutes per node
- **Tips**: Be careful when collecting — disturbing a hive at night summons aggressive bee swarms that deal significant damage. Daytime collection avoids this risk entirely.

## Rare Ingredient Farming Strategies

Rare ingredients cannot be efficiently farmed through gathering routes because their spawn rates are too low. Instead, they require specific activities.

### DragonSword Spice: Quest Completion Only

DragonSword Spice is obtained exclusively from completing the "Flame of the Ancients" Hero Request questline. Each completion yields one DragonSword Spice. This ingredient cannot be farmed through gathering or combat — it is a one-time reward. Use it only on master-tier recipes that provide the highest stat bonuses. For recipe recommendations, see our [best cooking recipes guide](/en/cooking-crafting/best-cooking-recipes/).

### Nameless Herb: Very Hard Dungeon Boss Drop

The Nameless Herb drops from Very Hard dungeon bosses at an estimated 10-15% rate according to community reports. The most efficient farming method is running the Crimson Hollow Very Hard repeatedly, as this dungeon has the fastest completion time for experienced teams. Expect to invest 3-5 hours of dungeon farming to accumulate enough Nameless Herbs for multiple master-tier meals.

### Abyssal Salt: Hidden Cave Collection

Abyssal Salt is found in hidden caves within the Crimson Hollow dungeon. Each dungeon run contains 2-3 Abyssal Salt nodes in semi-randomized cave locations. Thorough exploration of each dungeon run is necessary to find all Abyssal Salt deposits. Using the Stoneback Turtle Familiar helps detect hidden cave entrances through its passive detection ability.

## Farming Efficiency Tips: Maximizing Your Gathering Sessions

Beyond specific routes, several general principles improve farming efficiency:

1. **Stack activities**: Choose farming routes that produce multiple resource types simultaneously. The Glacial Rift route yields Frostpeak Truffles, Glacial Crystals, and Iron Ore in a single pass.
2. **Time your sessions**: Farm region-specific ingredients during their optimal conditions (night for Frostpeak Truffles, storms for Deep Sea Kelp).
3. **Cook before farming**: Consume a Skypeak Honeycake for movement speed to reduce travel time between gathering nodes.
4. **Rotate regions**: After completing a route in one region, move to another while the first region's nodes respawn. Alternating between Glacial Rift and volcanic region farming maximizes yield per hour.
5. **Use Familiars**: The Stoneback Turtle detects hidden nodes, the Frost Wisp charges Dana's gauge during passive farming, and the Abyssal Direwolf provides underwater speed. Match your Familiar to your farming activity.

## FAQ

### What is the most efficient ingredient to farm per hour?

Common ingredients like Orbis Herbs provide the highest quantity per hour (30-35 in 8 minutes), but they are also the least valuable. The most efficient valuable ingredient to farm is Frostpeak Truffles, which yield 6-10 per 20-minute pass and are needed for advanced and master-tier Stun Lock meals. Volcanic Chili is comparable in efficiency for DPS meal farming.

### Do ingredients expire or degrade over time?

No, ingredients in DragonSword: Awakening do not expire or degrade. Once gathered, they remain in your inventory indefinitely. This means you can stockpile ingredients during efficient farming sessions and use them weeks later without penalty. Bulk farming during optimal conditions and stockpiling for later use is the most efficient approach.

### Can I trade ingredients with other players?

DragonSword: Awakening does not have a player-to-player trading system as of launch. Ingredients are account-bound and cannot be traded. All ingredients must be personally gathered or earned through gameplay. This design ensures that every player engages with the gathering and exploration systems rather than bypassing them through trade.

### What is the fastest way to level cooking skill?

The fastest way to level cooking skill is to cook large quantities of intermediate-tier recipes using common ingredients. Spiced Game Skewers (Wild Game Meat + Mountain Salt + Orbis Herbs) are the most efficient recipe for skill leveling because they use only common ingredients and provide decent skill experience per cook. Mass-cooking Spiced Game Skewers can raise your cooking skill from level 1 to level 5 in approximately 30-40 minutes of cooking. For more on cooking skill, see our [cooking guide](/en/cooking-crafting/cooking-guide/).

### Are there ingredients only available through DLC?

No, all cooking ingredients are available through standard gameplay. DLC Familiars like the Valiant Hatchling and Abyssal Direwolf are separate from the ingredient system and do not provide exclusive ingredients. The only ingredients that might seem "gated" are rare materials like DragonSword Spice, which requires quest completion, but these quests are part of the base game, not DLC. For more on DLC vs free content, see our [buying guide](/en/buying-guide/buying-guide/).
