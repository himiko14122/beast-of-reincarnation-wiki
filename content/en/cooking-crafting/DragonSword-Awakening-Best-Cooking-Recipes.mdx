Knowing which meals to cook and when to eat them is one of the most impactful skills a DragonSword: Awakening player can develop. The cooking system offers over a dozen recipes across four quality tiers, each providing different stat bonuses that can dramatically enhance your hero's performance. Choosing the wrong meal — a defense buff when you are running a DPS Burst Lute build, for example — wastes both the meal and the time spent gathering its ingredients. This guide catalogs the best cooking recipes in the game, organized by build archetype and content type, so you always know exactly which meal to prepare before heading into a dungeon.

Every recipe listed here includes the exact ingredients required, the stat bonuses provided, the cooking level needed, and a recommendation for which heroes and builds benefit most. For the fundamentals of the cooking system, see our [cooking guide](/en/cooking-crafting/cooking-guide/).

## Best Recipes for DPS Burst Builds

DPS Burst builds on heroes like Lute and Roxy prioritize attack power, Signal Skill damage, and critical strike rate. The following meals provide the strongest bonuses for burst-damage gameplay.

| Recipe | Tier | Attack Power | Signal Skill Damage | Critical Strike Rate | Duration | Cooking Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volcanic Feast | Master | +20% | +10% | +5% | 45 min | 15 |
| Inferno Stew | Advanced | +15% | +8% | — | 30 min | 10 |
| Spiced Game Skewer | Intermediate | +10% | — | — | 20 min | 5 |

### Volcanic Feast (Master Tier)

The Volcanic Feast is the single best meal for DPS Burst builds in the entire game. It provides a +20% attack power bonus, a +10% Signal Skill damage increase, and a +5% critical strike rate — three effects that all directly amplify burst damage output. This meal requires cooking level 15 and uses one of each ingredient tier.

- **Ingredients**: Volcanic Chili × 2 (uncommon), DragonSword Spice × 1 (rare), Wild Game Meat × 3 (common)

- **Duration**: 45 minutes

- **Best for**: Lute DPS Burst, Roxy DPS Burst, any hero focused on maximizing Signal Skill damage

- **Cooking station**: Any level 3+ kitchen

The Volcanic Chili can be farmed in the volcanic region of the Continent of Orbis, while the DragonSword Spice is a one-time reward from completing the "Flame of the Ancients" Hero Request questline. Because DragonSword Spice is limited, save this recipe for your most important dungeon runs rather than casual farming.

### Inferno Stew (Advanced Tier)

If you do not have DragonSword Spice for the master-tier Volcanic Feast, the Inferno Stew is the best alternative. It provides +15% attack power and +8% Signal Skill damage without the critical strike bonus. This is still a powerful meal that can carry you through Hard dungeon content.

- **Ingredients**: Volcanic Chili × 1 (uncommon), Wild Game Meat × 2 (common), Mountain Salt × 1 (common)

- **Duration**: 30 minutes

- **Best for**: DPS Burst builds in Hard dungeon content

- **Cooking station**: Any level 2+ kitchen

The advantage of the Inferno Stew is that it uses only one uncommon ingredient and no rare ingredients, making it sustainable for repeated cooking. Farm Volcanic Chili for 15-20 minutes and you can produce 4-5 Inferno Stews.

### Spiced Game Skewer (Intermediate Tier)

For early game players who have not yet reached cooking level 10, the Spiced Game Skewer provides a solid +10% attack power bonus. It is the foundation of DPS meal preparation and should be your default meal until you unlock advanced recipes.

- **Ingredients**: Wild Game Meat × 2 (common), Mountain Salt × 1 (common), Orbis Herbs × 1 (common)

- **Duration**: 20 minutes

- **Best for**: Early game DPS Burst builds

- **Cooking station**: Any kitchen

This recipe uses only common ingredients, making it the most accessible DPS meal. You can cook Spiced Game Skewers indefinitely with ingredients gathered from standard exploration.

## Best Recipes for Stun Lock Builds

Stun Lock builds centered on Dana benefit from meals that enhance Frost Gauge charge rate and extend Status Ailment durations. These meals make the crowd-control chain more reliable by ensuring Freeze is available faster and lasts longer.

| Recipe | Tier | Frost Gauge Charge Rate | Ailment Duration | Damage Reduction | Duration | Cooking Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glacial Banquet | Master | +20% | +15% | +10% (while ailment active) | 45 min | 15 |
| Frostpeak Soup | Advanced | +15% | +10% | — | 30 min | 10 |
| Chilled Herb Salad | Intermediate | +10% | — | — | 20 min | 5 |

### Glacial Banquet (Master Tier)

The Glacial Banquet is the definitive meal for Stun Lock compositions. It provides +20% Frost Gauge charge rate, +15% ailment duration extension, and +10% damage reduction while any Status Ailment is active on the enemy. The combination of faster Freeze charging and longer ailment duration tightens the Stun Lock rotation, while the damage reduction provides survivability for the hero actively on the field.

- **Ingredients**: Frostpeak Truffle × 2 (uncommon), Nameless Herb × 1 (rare), Mountain Salt × 2 (common)

- **Duration**: 45 minutes

- **Best for**: Dana Stun Lock, any team running the Dana + Lute + Castella composition

- **Cooking station**: Any level 3+ kitchen

The Nameless Herb drops from Very Hard dungeon bosses at approximately 10-15% rate according to community reports, making this recipe expensive to produce repeatedly. Reserve it for progression attempts on your most challenging content.

### Frostpeak Soup (Advanced Tier)

The Frostpeak Soup is the sustainable alternative to the Glacial Banquet. It provides +15% Frost Gauge charge rate and +10% ailment duration — slightly less powerful but uses no rare ingredients.

- **Ingredients**: Frostpeak Truffle × 1 (uncommon), River Fish × 2 (common), Orbis Herbs × 1 (common)

- **Duration**: 30 minutes

- **Best for**: Regular Stun Lock dungeon farming

- **Cooking station**: Any level 2+ kitchen

Frostpeak Truffles can be farmed in the Glacial Rift region with approximately 15-20 minutes of dedicated gathering. This makes the Frostpeak Soup repeatable for daily dungeon farming.

### Chilled Herb Salad (Intermediate Tier)

For early game Stun Lock players, the Chilled Herb Salad provides +10% Frost Gauge charge rate. It is simple but effective for making Dana's Freeze more available during lower-level content.

- **Ingredients**: Frostpeak Truffle × 1 (uncommon), Orbis Herbs × 2 (common)

- **Duration**: 20 minutes

- **Best for**: Early game Stun Lock teams

- **Cooking station**: Any kitchen

## Best Recipes for Tank Builds

Tank builds on heroes like Johnny need defense, damage reduction, and health regeneration. These meals make tank heroes more resilient and allow them to stay on the field longer.

| Recipe | Tier | Defense | Damage Reduction | Health Regen | Movement Speed | Duration | Cooking Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abyssal Stew | Master | +20% | +15% | +10%/sec | — | 45 min | 15 |
| Ocean's Embrace | Advanced | +15% | +10% | — | — | 30 min | 10 |
| Skypeak Honeycake | Advanced | — | — | — | +15% | 30 min | 10 |
| Hearty Traveler's Stew | Intermediate | — | — | — | — | 20 min | 5 |

### Abyssal Stew (Master Tier)

The Abyssal Stew is the premier tank meal, providing +20% defense, +15% damage reduction, and +10% health regeneration per second. The health regeneration is particularly valuable because it allows Johnny to recover during tag rotations when other heroes are active.

- **Ingredients**: Deep Sea Kelp × 2 (uncommon), Abyssal Salt × 1 (rare), River Fish × 2 (common)

- **Duration**: 45 minutes

- **Best for**: Tank Johnny, any hero anchoring a defensive team composition

- **Cooking station**: Any level 3+ kitchen

Abyssal Salt is found in hidden caves within the Crimson Hollow dungeon, with limited spawns per run. This makes the Abyssal Stew expensive to produce and reserved for the most challenging content.

### Ocean's Embrace (Advanced Tier)

Ocean's Embrace provides +15% defense and +10% damage reduction without the health regeneration. It is the standard tank meal for regular content.

- **Ingredients**: Deep Sea Kelp × 1 (uncommon), River Fish × 2 (common), Mountain Salt × 1 (common)

- **Duration**: 30 minutes

- **Best for**: Regular tank dungeon farming

- **Cooking station**: Any level 2+ kitchen

## Best Recipes for Exploration and General Use

Not every meal needs to be optimized for combat. Some recipes provide bonuses that are more useful for open-world exploration and Familiar collection.

### Skypeak Honeycake (Advanced Tier)

The Skypeak Honeycake provides +15% movement speed and +10% ailment duration. The movement speed bonus is invaluable for exploring the open world, running between gathering nodes, and reaching remote Familiar habitats.

- **Ingredients**: Skypeak Honey × 2 (uncommon), Orbis Herbs × 1 (common), Mountain Salt × 1 (common)

- **Duration**: 30 minutes

- **Best for**: Open-world exploration, Familiar collection routes

- **Cooking station**: Any level 2+ kitchen

For detailed Familiar collection routes, see our [Familiar collection route guide](/en/familiars/familiar-collection-route/).

### Hearty Traveler's Stew (Intermediate Tier)

The Hearty Traveler's Stew provides +10% experience gain and +5% Gold find rate. While the combat bonuses are minimal, the experience and Gold bonuses make this the best meal for leveling and resource farming.

- **Ingredients**: Wild Game Meat × 1 (common), River Fish × 1 (common), Orbis Herbs × 2 (common)

- **Duration**: 20 minutes

- **Best for**: Leveling, Gold farming, general overworld play

- **Cooking station**: Any kitchen

## Recipe Unlocking: How to Discover New Recipes

Recipes in DragonSword: Awakening are not all available from the start. They are unlocked through four methods:

1. **Cooking Level Milestones**: Reaching cooking levels 5, 10, and 15 automatically unlocks a set of recipes at each tier.

2. **Hero Request Quests**: Completing specific hero questlines rewards unique recipes. For example, completing Dana's questline unlocks the Frostpeak Soup recipe.

3. **Exploration Discovery**: Finding recipe scrolls hidden in caves, underwater areas, and remote locations. These scrolls teach one recipe each when used at a kitchen station.

4. **NPC Friendship**: Building friendship with specific NPCs through dialogue choices and gift-giving can unlock recipes. The cooking NPC at the Red Fox base camp teaches one new recipe at friendship level 3 and another at friendship level 5.

The recipe discovery system encourages players to explore thoroughly and engage with NPCs, tying cooking progression directly into the open-world exploration loop. For more on the crafting side of the system, see our [crafting materials guide](/en/cooking-crafting/crafting-materials-guide/).

## FAQ

### What is the best overall meal in DragonSword Awakening?

There is no single best meal — the best meal depends on your build and content. For DPS Burst, the Volcanic Feast is the strongest. For Stun Lock, the Glacial Banquet is unmatched. For tanks, the Abyssal Stew provides the best survivability. Always match your meal to your build archetype rather than chasing a generic "best" option.

### Can I cook master-tier recipes without rare ingredients?

No, all master-tier recipes require at least one rare ingredient (DragonSword Spice, Nameless Herb, or Abyssal Salt). However, advanced-tier recipes are nearly as effective and use only uncommon and common ingredients. The stat difference between advanced and master tiers is approximately 5% on the primary stat bonus, which is meaningful but not game-breaking.

### How do I farm DragonSword Spice?

DragonSword Spice cannot be farmed — it is a one-time reward from completing the "Flame of the Ancients" Hero Request questline. Each completion yields one DragonSword Spice. Because this ingredient is limited, use it strategically on the Volcanic Feast or other master-tier recipes for your most important progression attempts. For more on limited resources, see our [Nameless Soul guide](/en/cooking-crafting/nameless-soul-guide/).

### Do cooking skill levels affect meal quality?

Yes, your cooking skill level determines which quality tiers you can access. Level 1 allows basic meals, level 5 unlocks intermediate, level 10 unlocks advanced, and level 15 unlocks master-tier. Additionally, higher cooking levels provide a small chance to produce a "perfect" meal that provides 110% of the normal stat bonus. This perfect meal chance increases by approximately 2% per cooking level.

### Are there recipes that affect Familiars?

Currently, no recipes directly boost Familiar abilities. However, the Skypeak Honeycake's movement speed bonus indirectly helps with Familiar collection by allowing you to reach Familiar habitats faster. Hound13 may introduce Familiar-specific recipes in future updates through the Rift System. Check our [best Familiars guide](/en/familiars/best-familiars-combat-guide/) for the latest information on Familiar abilities.
