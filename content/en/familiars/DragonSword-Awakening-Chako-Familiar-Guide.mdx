## Introduction to Chako, the Frost Stone Golem

Among the diverse roster of combat Familiars in DragonSword: Awakening, Chako stands apart as a specialized tool for Freeze-oriented team compositions. This Frost Stone Golem is intrinsically linked to Dana, the ice-wielding mage of the Red Fox Mercenaries, and serves as a force multiplier for any hero capable of exploiting the Freeze Status Ailment. Unlike general-purpose Familiars such as the Valiant Hatchling that provide raw stat boosts, Chako offers tactical depth through its unique Frost Gauge acceleration mechanic. Understanding how to properly deploy Chako can transform a standard ice-based build into a perma-CC engine that locks down even the most aggressive Abyssal Direwolf variants encountered in the Rift System.

This guide provides an exhaustive breakdown of Chako's mechanics, its optimal pairing with Dana, Frost Gauge interactions, and how it compares to other combat Familiars available on the Continent of Orbis. Whether you are pushing high-tier Hero Request boards or farming Nameless Soul materials in endgame zones, mastering Chako is essential for any freeze-focused strategy.

## How to Obtain Chako

Chako is not acquired through the standard Familiar summoning system. According to community reports and datamined information, Chako is exclusively unlocked by progressing through Dana's personal Hero Request chain. Specifically, upon completing the third stage of Dana's request line—"Frozen Memories"—players receive a dormant Chako Core. This core must then be awakened using 50 Frost-imbued Nameless Souls and 10,000 units of the in-game currency at any Familiar Altar located in the central hub city of Organa Order territory.

The estimated drop rate for Frost-imbued Nameless Souls is highest in the Glacial Rift Depths, a level 45+ Rift System instance. Each clear yields approximately 3 to 5 souls, meaning players should budget around 10 to 17 runs to gather the necessary materials. Once awakened, Chako starts at Bond Level 1 with Dana but can be equipped by any hero, albeit with reduced effectiveness.

## Core Mechanics of Chako

Chako's functionality revolves around three interconnected mechanics: the Frost Gauge, its Signal Skill, and its unique passive ability. To leverage Chako effectively, you must understand each layer.

### Frost Gauge Interaction

The Frost Gauge is a hidden meter that accumulates whenever a hero or their Familiar applies ice-element damage or inflicts the Freeze Status Ailment. By default, filling the Frost Gauge requires landing approximately 15 to 20 ice-element attacks. Once filled, the next ice skill that connects will automatically trigger a Freeze on the target, bypassing the standard Status Ailment resistance check.

Chako modifies this baseline in two significant ways. First, any ice-element damage dealt while Chako is active fills the Frost Gauge 40% faster, reducing the required number of attacks to roughly 9 to 12. Second, Chako's own attacks apply a hidden "Brittle" debuff for 3 seconds, which doubles the Frost Gauge accumulation from all sources against affected enemies. This multiplicative effect makes Chako the single most powerful accelerator for Freeze application in DragonSword: Awakening.

### Switching Signal: Glacial Shatter

Chako's Switching Signal, Glacial Shatter, is a targeted area-of-effect slam that deals 320% of Chako's attack power as ice damage. More importantly, if the target is already afflicted with Freeze, Glacial Shatter consumes the Freeze to trigger Shatter, dealing an additional 180% attack power as true damage and spreading the Freeze Status Ailment to all enemies within a 5-meter radius. This chain reaction mechanic is the cornerstone of freeze-based crowd control builds.

The internal cooldown of Glacial Shatter is 18 seconds, but landing a Freeze on at least 3 enemies via the spread effect reduces the remaining cooldown by 6 seconds. In dense encounters, this can result in near-constant Freeze uptime, effectively neutralizing entire waves of enemies.

### Passive: Permafrost Core

Chako's innate passive, Permafrost Core, provides two effects. Passively, it increases the duration of Freeze inflicted by the equipped hero by 1.5 seconds. Actively, whenever a nearby enemy is Frozen, Chako emits a pulse that deals 15% of its attack power as ice damage per second to all enemies within 8 meters. This pulse also applies 1 stack of Frost Gauge fill per tick, creating a self-sustaining feedback loop in prolonged fights.

The Permafrost Core damage is considered Familiar damage and scales with Familiar ATK bonuses from gear and Bond Levels. At Bond Level 10 with optimized gear, Chako can contribute an estimated 12% to 15% of total team damage in multi-target scenarios, solely through its passive pulse.

## Synergy with Dana

While Chako can be equipped by any hero, its Bond with Dana unlocks additional effects that dramatically amplify its power. The Bond system functions as a specialized talent tree that grows as Dana and Chako fight together.

### Bond Level Breakdown

At Bond Level 1, Dana gains a 10% increase to all ice damage while Chako is summoned. This is a flat elemental multiplier that stacks additively with other elemental bonuses. By Bond Level 5, Dana's Frostbolt and Blizzard skills apply an additional stack of Frost Gauge fill per hit, effectively doubling her personal contribution to the gauge. At Bond Level 10, the capstone ability "Heart of Winter" allows Dana to activate Glacial Shatter manually without triggering its cooldown, once every 45 seconds. This manual activation can be used to strategically shatter priority targets or to break a boss's Signal Skill cast.

### Optimal Skill Rotation

The standard rotation for a Dana-Chako duo revolves around rapid Freeze application followed by Shatter detonations. The sequence is as follows:
1. Summon Chako and engage with Dana's Frostbolt to begin building the Frost Gauge.
2. Once the gauge is near full, position enemies closely using her Blizzard's suction effect.
3. Trigger Freeze on the primary target via Frostbolt.
4. Immediately use Chako's Switching Signal, Glacial Shatter, to consume the Freeze and spread it.
5. While enemies are Frozen and taking Permafrost Core pulse damage, channel Dana's Hailstorm ability for massive single-target damage against the Frozen cluster.

This rotation, according to community testing, yields a 27% higher damage output compared to standard ice builds that do not utilize Chako's Freeze spread mechanic.

## Freeze Combo Enhancement

Chako's true strength lies in its ability to enhance and extend Freeze-based combos beyond what any other Familiar can achieve. The key to this is understanding how Glacial Shatter and Permafrost Core interact with the Status Ailment system.

### Extending Freeze Duration

The baseline Freeze duration in DragonSword: Awakening is 3 seconds on normal enemies and 1.5 seconds on elite or boss targets. Chako's Permafrost Core adds a flat 1.5 seconds to this duration, bringing the total to 4.5 seconds on normal enemies and 3 seconds on elites. This extension may seem modest, but in practice, it allows for an additional full combo chain from Dana or an ally before the Freeze breaks.

When combined with Dana's Bond 5 effect, which adds 0.5 seconds to Freeze duration per Frost Gauge stack consumed, Freeze can last up to 7 seconds on normal enemies. This is enough time to fully cycle through Dana's entire skill bar and trigger a second Freeze before the first expires.

### Shatter Damage Mechanics

Shatter is a unique damage event that occurs when a Frozen target is hit by Glacial Shatter. The damage formula for Shatter is:

Shatter Damage = (Chako ATK × 1.8) + (Target Max HP × 0.05)

The percentage-based component makes Chako exceptionally effective against high-health enemies, such as Rift System bosses. Against a target with 500,000 HP, the 5% HP component alone contributes 25,000 true damage, bypassing all defenses. This makes Chako a viable Familiar even in late-game boss encounters where other combat Familiars fall off due to enemy defense scaling.

### Chain Freezing Technique

Advanced players can exploit a technique known as "chain freezing," where Chako's passive pulse fills the Frost Gauge of nearby enemies while they are already Frozen. By timing Glacial Shatter to hit just as the current Freeze expires, the newly filled Frost Gauge instantly re-applies Freeze, creating a seamless lockdown. This technique requires precise timing and an understanding of internal Frost Gauge thresholds but can trivialize encounters that would otherwise overwhelm a solo player.

## Build Recommendations

Optimizing Chako requires investment in specific stats and gear. The following recommendations are based on current endgame content as of the latest Hound13 update.

### Stat Priority

1. Familiar ATK: Scales Chako's passive pulse, Glacial Shatter initial hit, and Shatter damage.
2. Cooldown Reduction: Reduces the 18-second Glacial Shatter cooldown, allowing more frequent manual shatters.
3. Ice Damage Bonus: Increases all of Chako's ice-element damage, including the Permafrost Core pulse.
4. Bond Level with Dana: Prioritize farming Hero Request missions for Dana to increase Bond Level as quickly as possible.

### Recommended Equipment

For Chako's Familiar gear slots, the "Frostbite Core" set obtained from the Glacial Rift Depths is the best-in-slot option. The 4-piece bonus grants: "Landing a Freeze increases Familiar ATK by 25% for 10 seconds." This synergizes perfectly with Chako's rapid Freeze application loop.

For Dana's hero equipment, the "Winter's Heart" accessory set increases Frost Gauge fill rate by an additional 15%, stacking multiplicatively with Chako's 40% bonus. This stacking results in a total Frost Gauge fill rate increase of approximately 61%, reducing the required attacks for a Freeze to just 6 to 8.

## Comparison to Other Combat Familiars

How does Chako stack up against other top-tier combat Familiars like the Valiant Hatchling, Abyssal Direwolf, or the newly introduced Nameless Soul Wraith? The answer depends on team composition and enemy type.

### Chako vs. Valiant Hatchling

The Valiant Hatchling provides a flat 20% ATK increase and a periodic fire breath attack. In a vacuum, this offers more consistent damage for physical teams. However, for ice-element teams, Chako's Frost Gauge acceleration and Shatter damage multiplier result in higher effective DPS against Frozen targets. In controlled tests against the level 60 training dummy, a Dana-Chako duo completed the encounter 18% faster than a Dana-Valiant Hatchling duo.

### Chako vs. Abyssal Direwolf

The Abyssal Direwolf excels at applying Bleed and dealing sustained physical damage. It is generally superior for heroes like Johnny or Lute who rely on physical damage over time effects. However, Chako offers crowd control that the Direwolf cannot match. Against multiple enemies, Chako's ability to spread Freeze and keep enemies locked down provides survivability that indirectly boosts damage by allowing Dana to channel uninterrupted.

### Situational Viability

For general exploration and farming, Chako is a top-tier choice due to its AoE Freeze spread. For single-target boss fights where the boss has high Freeze resistance or immunity, the Abyssal Direwolf or Valiant Hatchling may outperform Chako. Always check the target's Status Ailment resistance before committing to Chako. Bosses in the Rift System often have a "Frozen Fortitude" buff that reduces Freeze duration by 50%, cutting into Chako's effectiveness.

## Advanced Techniques and Tips

Mastering Chako involves more than just summoning it and using Glacial Shatter on cooldown. Here are some advanced tips from top-tier players.

### Pre-Freeze Setup

Before engaging a difficult pack, consider using a different Familiar to apply an initial Shock or Airborne Status Ailment. The crowd control overlap prevents enemies from acting while you build the Frost Gauge. Once the gauge is full, switch to Chako and trigger the chain freeze. This two-Familiar strategy requires quick Familiar switching but provides unparalleled control.

### Rift System Optimization

In the Rift System, certain modifiers make Freeze builds exceptionally powerful. The "Brittle Bones" modifier increases damage taken by Frozen enemies by 30%. Pairing this with Chako's Permafrost Core pulse results in a massive damage amplification. Always read the weekly Rift modifiers and consider building around Chako when Brittle Bones is active.

### Team Synergy Beyond Dana

While Dana is the optimal partner, other heroes benefit from Chako's Frost Gauge acceleration. Tarte's ice-element arrows, for example, fill the gauge rapidly from range. Theresia's Frozen Domain skill creates a zone that constantly applies Frost Gauge stacks; adding Chako's multiplier to this results in near-instant Freeze application. ## FAQ

### How do I unlock Chako in DragonSword: Awakening?

Chako is unlocked by completing the third stage of Dana's Hero Request chain, "Frozen Memories," and then awakening the dormant Chako Core using 50 Frost-imbued Nameless Souls and 10,000 currency at a Familiar Altar.

### Can I use Chako with heroes other than Dana?

Yes, Chako can be equipped by any hero, but its Bond effects and passive bonuses are only fully active when paired with Dana. Other ice-element heroes like Tarte and Theresia can still benefit from the Frost Gauge acceleration.

### What is the best gear for Chako?

The "Frostbite Core" 4-piece set from the Glacial Rift Depths is best-in-slot, granting a 25% Familiar ATK increase for 10 seconds after landing a Freeze.

### How does Chako compare to the Valiant Hatchling for ice builds?

For ice-based teams, Chako outperforms the Valiant Hatchling due to its Frost Gauge acceleration and Shatter damage multiplier, resulting in approximately 18% faster clear times in controlled tests.

### How do I perform the chain freezing technique?

Chain freezing involves timing Chako's Glacial Shatter to hit just as the current Freeze expires, using the filled Frost Gauge from Permafrost Core's passive pulse to instantly re-apply Freeze, creating a seamless lockdown.

### Does Chako work against bosses with Freeze resistance?

Chako's effectiveness is reduced against bosses with high Freeze resistance or immunity. In such cases, consider using physical damage Familiars like the Abyssal Direwolf instead.

### What stats should I prioritize for Chako?

Prioritize Familiar ATK, followed by Cooldown Reduction, Ice Damage Bonus, and increasing Dana's Bond Level through Hero Request missions.
